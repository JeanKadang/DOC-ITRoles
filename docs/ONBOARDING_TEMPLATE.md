## 30-day goals — Learn

**Theme:** Understand the team, systems, and context. Listen more than you act.

### Organizational orientation

- [ ] Complete company induction (HR, security, values, ways of working)
- [ ] Meet all direct teammates (1:1 with each within first 2 weeks)
- [ ] Meet key stakeholders across adjacent domains (see Interactions section of role definition)
- [ ] Understand team rituals: standups, sprint ceremonies, architecture reviews, on-call rotation

### Technical orientation

- [ ] Read the role definition fully — identify any skill gaps to discuss with manager
- [ ] Review existing architecture documentation, decision records (ADRs), and runbooks for the domain
- [ ] Get hands-on access to key systems and environments (non-production first)
- [ ] Shadow at least 2 live tasks or incidents to understand how the team operates
- [ ] Review [Career Paths & Skills Progression](SKILLS_PROGRESSION.md) and [Domain Interactions](CROSS_DOMAIN_INTERACTIONS.md)

### Domain-specific orientation

- [ ] \<Add 3–5 domain-specific tasks — e.g., "Review current Azure landing zone design", "Read the Kubernetes platform runbook"\>

### 30-day check-in (with manager)

- What's clear and what's still unclear?
- Are tools and access working?
- Any immediate blockers or concerns?
- Agree on initial focus area for days 31–60

## 60-day goals — Contribute

**Theme:** Start delivering independently. Take ownership of a scoped task or workstream.

### Delivery

- [ ] Own and complete at least one meaningful task end-to-end (scoped appropriately for the level)
- [ ] Participate actively in architecture reviews, design sessions, or sprint planning
- [ ] Contribute to team documentation: update, correct, or create at least one artefact
- [ ] Raise at least one improvement idea or gap identified during the learn phase

### Relationships

- [ ] Established working relationships with at least 3 cross-domain stakeholders
- [ ] Buddy relationship productive — giving and receiving feedback
- [ ] Comfortable raising blockers and asking for help promptly

### Technical depth

- [ ] Proficient with core team tooling (see Key Technologies in role definition)
- [ ] Understand the team's incident response process; capable of joining the on-call rotation (if applicable)
- [ ] Working knowledge of relevant compliance and governance requirements for the domain

### 60-day check-in (with manager)

- Are initial goals met? What slipped and why?
- Feedback from buddy and at least one stakeholder
- Confirm certification or learning path targets (see role definition)
- Adjust 90-day goals if needed

---

## 90-day goals — Own

**Theme:** Operating as a full team member. Delivering independently and improving the team around you.

### Delivery

- [ ] Delivered at least one significant piece of work — design, implementation, or process improvement
- [ ] Contributing to sprint goals with minimal oversight
- [ ] On-call or operational rotation joined (if applicable to the role)

### Standards and quality

- [ ] Familiar with all relevant team standards, reference architectures, and governance frameworks
- [ ] Code/design reviews: giving meaningful feedback, not just approvals
- [ ] Documentation maintained and updated as part of normal workflow

### Growth

- [ ] Progress on at least one certification from the role's recommended path
- [ ] Have a documented development plan for the next 6–12 months
- [ ] Identified a specific area to deepen expertise or own going forward

### 90-day review (with manager)

- Full performance review against 30/60/90 goals
- Agree on medium-term objectives (OKRs or equivalent)
- Confirm ongoing development path and any training investment needed
- Document any outstanding gaps and a plan to address them
