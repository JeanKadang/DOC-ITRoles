# Data & AI

## Purpose

This chapter owns the organisation's data and artificial intelligence capabilities — from data platform architecture and engineering pipelines through to storage management, database reliability, and AI governance. It ensures data is treated as a strategic asset through robust DataOps, data mesh principles, and responsible AI frameworks. The chapter drives trustworthy, governed, and scalable data and AI solutions that deliver measurable business value.

## Key cross-chapter collaborations

- Partners with **Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure** for shared platform services including AI platform infrastructure, MLOps tooling, cloud storage, and the compute environments that power data workloads.
- Works with **Security & Identity** to apply data classification, access controls, and data protection policies that safeguard sensitive data assets and meet regulatory obligations.
- Collaborates with **Service & Governance** on enterprise architecture alignment, ensuring data and AI solutions conform to data governance standards, architectural guardrails, and service management processes.
