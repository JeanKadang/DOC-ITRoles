# Service & Governance

## Purpose

This chapter owns the frameworks, processes, and architectural standards that enable the organisation to deliver technology services in a consistent, governed, and strategically aligned manner. It encompasses ITSM, CMDB and configuration management, service management frameworks, infrastructure onboarding standards, and enterprise architecture governance. The chapter ensures that all technology delivery is underpinned by sound architectural thinking and effective service management practice.

## Key cross-chapter collaborations

- Acts as an architectural authority across all chapters — providing enterprise architecture guardrails, solution architecture review, and strategic alignment for initiatives led by every other chapter.
- Partners with **Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure** and **DevOps & Delivery** to define and enforce infrastructure onboarding standards, change management processes, and CMDB accuracy.
- Works with **Data & AI** and **Security & Identity** to ensure that governance frameworks adequately address data governance obligations and security compliance requirements.
