# Leadership

## Purpose

This chapter encompasses the executive and cross-cutting technical leadership roles that connect, coordinate, and drive excellence across the entire organisation. It includes the C-Suite executives (CEO, CTO, CIO), the SVP of Technology, the CISO, six Chapter Leads, the Technical Area Lead (TAL), the Product Area Lead (PAL), the Engineering Practices Champion, and the Technical Community Leader. These roles set organisational strategy, provide technical direction, foster engineering culture, and ensure coherent execution across the full technology estate.

## Key cross-chapter collaborations

- C-Suite executives set organisation-wide strategy, investment priorities, and governance frameworks — all chapters operate within the direction they establish.
- Chapter Leads serve as the primary integration points between their respective chapters and the broader leadership community — cascading strategy, surfacing cross-chapter dependencies, and driving consistency in engineering practices.
- The Engineering Practices Champion and Technical Community Leader operate across all chapters, cultivating shared standards, communities of practice, and a culture of continuous improvement.
- The TAL and PAL provide organisational alignment between technology delivery and business objectives, ensuring that all seven chapters are working toward coherent outcomes.
