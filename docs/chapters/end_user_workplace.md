# End User & Workplace

## Purpose

This chapter owns the digital workplace experience, client device engineering, endpoint management capabilities, and the service desk that enable employees to work securely and productively. It covers client OS engineering and device imaging (Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux desktop), hardware lifecycle management for the Lenovo-primary fleet (ThinkPad, ThinkCentre, with MacBook and Linux-on-Lenovo also in scope), device management platforms (Intune, SCCM), Microsoft 365 governance, and Tier-1/2/3 end-user support — ensuring that the modern workplace is well-governed, secure, and aligned to user needs. The chapter balances productivity, security, and user experience across a diverse device and application estate.

## Key cross-chapter collaborations

- Partners with **Security & Identity** to enforce endpoint compliance policies, conditional access controls, and identity-driven device trust as part of a zero-trust security model.
- Works with **Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure** to align workplace platforms with broader cloud strategy, including Microsoft 365 tenant governance and hybrid identity integration.
- Collaborates with **Service & Governance** on ITSM processes, configuration management, and service delivery standards that govern endpoint and workplace service operations.
