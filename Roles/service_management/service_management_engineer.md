## Role Overview

The Service Management Engineer implements and maintains IT service management platforms and processes across the organization. This role focuses on configuring ITSM tools, automating workflows, and supporting service management processes to ensure efficient IT service delivery.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Team — execution of ITSM platform configuration and process implementation tasks to defined standards
- **Experience Anchor:** 1-3 years in ITSM/service management engineering — works under guidance, building toward independent delivery
- **Out of Scope:** ITSM platform architecture (Senior Engineer/Architect-owned); process design (Process Owners-owned); backlog prioritisation (Product Owner-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Service Management Senior Engineers — complex implementation issues and design-level questions
- **Escalated To By:** stakeholders on service management need clarification

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Implements and maintains ITSM platform configurations, workflows, and service catalogue items ensuring efficient IT service delivery through reliable, well-documented service management processes and tooling
- **Value Metrics:** ITSM configuration quality, workflow automation success rate, service catalog effectiveness, documentation completeness, user satisfaction with ITSM tools
- **Key Stakeholders:** Service Management Senior Engineers, process owners, service desk staff, knowledge management teams, ITSM Product Owner
- **Processes Supported:** ITSM platform module configuration, service catalog development, workflow and notification setup, CMDB maintenance, report and dashboard creation, ITSM incident and request support

## Key Responsibilities

- Implement and configure ITSM platform modules and features
- Create and maintain workflows for service management processes
- Build and customize service catalogs and request forms
- Configure CMDB and asset management systems
- Implement reporting dashboards and metrics
- Support incident, problem, and change management processes
- Troubleshoot and resolve ITSM platform issues
- Maintain documentation for ITSM configurations and procedures

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| ITSM platform module configuration, service catalog item development, and workflow automation execution | Architecture decisions and process design |
| ITSM documentation maintenance, runbooks, and operational configuration records | Process design, SLA target setting, and enhancement prioritisation |
| ITSM platform troubleshooting, incident triage, and user support for service management tooling | Platform upgrade strategy and advanced customisation design |

- **Awareness level expected:** Azure Monitor/SCOM (monitoring integration), AI-driven ITSM automation
