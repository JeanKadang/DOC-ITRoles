## Role Overview

The Service Management Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of complex IT service management solutions across the organization. This role provides technical leadership for ITSM platform configurations, workflows, and integrations while working closely with process owners to translate service management requirements into effective implementations.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — detailed engineering design and delivery within the ITSM reference architecture
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in ITSM/service management engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's reference architecture
- **Out of Scope:** ITSM platform architecture and process reference models (Architect-owned); business process/SLA target-setting (Process Owners-owned); platform commercial decisions (Product Owner-owned)
- **Escalates To:** ITSM Architect — architecture-level questions and design direction
- **Escalated To By:** Service Management Engineers on complex implementation issues

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads complex ITSM platform implementations, workflow automation, and enterprise system integrations ensuring service management processes deliver consistent IT service quality and operational efficiency
- **Value Metrics:** ITSM platform availability, workflow automation coverage, complex incident resolution time, integration reliability, platform upgrade success rate
- **Key Stakeholders:** ITSM Architect, process owners, Service Management Product Owner, service desk teams, integration engineers
- **Processes Supported:** Complex ITSM platform implementation, advanced workflow and automation design, enterprise integration, CMDB standards, platform upgrades and migrations, engineer mentorship

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex ITSM platform solutions
- Develop advanced workflows and automation for service management
- Lead troubleshooting for critical ITSM platform issues
- Create and maintain ITSM platform standards and best practices
- Implement integrations between ITSM and other enterprise systems
- Optimize ITSM platform performance and user experience
- Plan and execute ITSM platform upgrades and migrations
- Provide technical mentorship to Service Management Engineers

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Complex ITSM platform implementation standards, advanced workflow automation, and platform integration design | Architecture decisions and ITSM governance framework |
| ITSM platform performance optimisation, upgrade planning, and technical escalation management | Product roadmap priorities and platform procurement decisions |
| Service catalog technical design, CMDB configuration standards, and code review quality control | Service level agreement target-setting and process design |
