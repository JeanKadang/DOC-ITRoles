## Role Overview

The Vendor / Supplier / IT Asset Manager owns the lifecycle of the organization's IT vendor relationships, software licensing, and hardware/software asset inventory. This role ensures the organization knows what technology assets it owns and licenses, tracks vendor contracts and renewal dates, and manages supplier performance and risk — providing the commercial and inventory discipline that technical teams depend on but do not own themselves.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Organization-wide — owns the IT Asset Management (ITAM) and Software Asset Management (SAM) function and the vendor contract register across all technology domains.
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in IT asset management, vendor management, or IT procurement; expected to operate independently across vendor negotiations and audits.
- **Out of Scope:** Does not own technology selection or architecture decisions (owned by the relevant domain Architect); does not own the FinOps cloud cost optimization function (owned by FinOps roles, though this role provides the license/contract inputs FinOps consumes); does not own procurement legal/contract terms (owned by Procurement/Legal, this role provides the technical and commercial requirements).
- **Escalates To:** Service Management Architect (for vendor disputes, major contract risk, or software license compliance exposure requiring executive attention).
- **Escalated To By:** Any domain Architect or Engineer (when a vendor relationship or license constraint blocks a technical decision).

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Minimize unnecessary software and hardware spend, eliminate license compliance risk, and ensure vendor performance is actively managed against contracted service levels.
- **Value Metrics:** Software license compliance rate, unused/underutilized license reclamation savings, vendor contract renewal on-time rate, vendor SLA adherence, IT asset inventory accuracy.
- **Key Stakeholders:** FinOps Architect/Engineer (cloud and license cost inputs), Procurement/Legal, CFO's office (budget and audit), every domain Architect (as a technology consumer and vendor relationship stakeholder).
- **Processes Supported:** Software asset management (SAM), hardware asset lifecycle tracking, vendor contract management and renewal, software license true-up and audits, vendor performance/SLA reviews.

## Key Responsibilities

- Maintain the authoritative IT asset inventory (hardware and software) across the organization, reconciling discovery tooling data with procurement and finance records.
- Own the software license register, tracking entitlements, deployed usage, and compliance position for every licensed product in the estate.
- Manage the vendor contract calendar, ensuring renewals, terminations, and renegotiations are actioned well ahead of expiry to avoid unplanned lapses or auto-renewal at unfavorable terms.
- Lead software license true-up processes and respond to vendor compliance audits, coordinating with Procurement/Legal and the relevant technical teams.
- Identify and reclaim unused or underutilized software licenses and hardware assets, feeding savings opportunities to FinOps and Finance.
- Conduct regular vendor performance reviews against contracted SLAs, escalating chronic underperformance to Procurement/Legal for contract remediation.
- Partner with domain Architects during technology selection to provide licensing cost, contract term, and vendor risk input before a purchase decision is finalized.
- Maintain the vendor risk register, tracking financial stability, security posture (in partnership with the Security Architect), and business continuity risk for critical suppliers.
- Support hardware asset lifecycle management — procurement, deployment tracking, and disposal/decommissioning in coordination with Endpoint Management and Server Hardware teams.
- Produce regular IT asset and vendor spend reporting for Finance and executive leadership.

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| IT asset inventory accuracy, software license register, and compliance position | Technology selection and architecture decisions (owned by the relevant domain Architect) |
| Vendor contract renewal calendar and renewal/termination recommendations | Final contract legal terms and negotiation (owned by Procurement/Legal) |
| License reclamation and underutilization identification | Cloud cost optimization strategy (owned by FinOps, this role supplies license data as an input) |
| Vendor performance/SLA review findings and risk register | Vendor selection for net-new technology purchases (owned jointly with the requesting Architect and Procurement) |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Strong working knowledge of IT Asset Management (ITAM) and Software Asset Management (SAM) frameworks and tooling (ServiceNow ITAM/SAM, Flexera, Snow Software, Ivanti).
- Ability to read and interpret vendor license agreements and entitlement terms (per-seat, per-core, subscription, consumption-based) well enough to assess compliance position.
- Familiarity with software discovery and inventory reconciliation techniques across on-premises and cloud/SaaS estates.
- Vendor contract lifecycle management, including renewal negotiation preparation and SLA performance tracking.
- Data analysis skills for license utilization reporting and spend/compliance dashboards.

**Soft Skills & Leadership:**

- Strong negotiation and relationship management skills for vendor renewal and dispute conversations.
- Cross-functional collaboration with Procurement, Legal, Finance, and every technical domain.
- Detail-oriented and methodical approach to inventory and license compliance record-keeping.

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

**Expert level required:**

- ServiceNow ITAM/SAM or Flexera (software asset management)
- Vendor contract and license entitlement management

**Proficient level required:**

- Snow Software or Ivanti IT Asset Management
- Cloud cost/license reporting tooling (in partnership with FinOps)

**Working Knowledge required:**

- Software discovery/inventory reconciliation tooling
- Endpoint and server hardware lifecycle processes

**Awareness level expected:**

- The underlying architecture of major platforms this role tracks licensing for

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Reconciling software discovery data against the license register to identify a potential compliance gap.
- Reviewing the vendor contract calendar and initiating a renewal conversation for a contract expiring in 90 days.
- Responding to a vendor's license true-up audit request, coordinating data collection with the relevant technical team.
- Identifying a batch of unused software licenses and submitting a reclamation recommendation to FinOps.
- Conducting a quarterly vendor performance review against SLA commitments for a critical supplier.
- Providing licensing cost and contract term input to an Architect evaluating a new observability platform.
- Updating the vendor risk register following a supplier's public security incident disclosure.
- Producing the monthly IT asset and vendor spend report for the CFO's office.

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible — the role is tooling and relationship-based with no physical infrastructure dependency.
- **Collaboration Tools:** ServiceNow ITAM/SAM, Flexera/Snow Software, Power BI, Microsoft Teams, Confluence.
- **On-Site Requirements:** None typically; occasional on-site for vendor business reviews.
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Low to moderate — primarily standard business hours, with some flexibility for vendor calls across time zones.
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on a rotating on-call schedule; expected to respond promptly to urgent license compliance or vendor contract issues.
