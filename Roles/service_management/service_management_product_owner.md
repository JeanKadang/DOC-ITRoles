## Role Overview

The Service Management Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's IT service management capabilities. This role leads a team of ITSM specialists, ensuring that service management processes, tools, and practices meet business requirements, operational standards, and continual improvement objectives.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — ITSM product backlog, service management roadmap, and service catalog portfolio governance
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Architecture decisions and technical ITSM platform design (Architect-owned); ITSM platform commercial procurement and licensing decisions (Procurement-owned); enterprise service architecture direction and governance standards (Enterprise Architect-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Service & Governance Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** ITSM Architect on roadmap and architecture-alignment questions

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Manages the ITSM service portfolio and platform backlog ensuring service management capabilities evolve to meet operational requirements, improve user satisfaction, and deliver measurable efficiency improvements across the organisation
- **Value Metrics:** Service availability, time-to-resolution, customer satisfaction (CSAT), self-service adoption rate, ITSM roadmap delivery velocity, service catalog maturity progression
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT leadership, process owners, service desk management, business relationship managers, ITSM Architect
- **Processes Supported:** ITSM backlog management, service catalog governance, SLA definition and tracking, process automation prioritisation, stakeholder reporting, agile ceremonies

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for ITSM capabilities
- Prioritize service management initiatives based on business impact
- Define acceptance criteria for ITSM deliverables and enhancements
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the ITSM team
- Engage with stakeholders to understand service management requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for ITSM evolution
- Manage service catalog and portfolio development
- Track and report on service performance metrics

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| ITSM product backlog, service management roadmap, and service catalog portfolio governance | Architecture decisions and technical ITSM platform design |
| Service SLA targets, self-service adoption programme, and ITSM performance reporting | ITSM platform commercial procurement and licensing decisions |
| ITSM continual improvement programme and team capability development priorities | Enterprise service architecture direction and governance standards |

- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, ServiceNow dashboards, Power BI
