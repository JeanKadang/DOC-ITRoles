## Role Overview

The FinOps Architect designs and implements cloud financial management strategies, governance frameworks, and cost optimization architectures across the organization. This role bridges financial management practices with cloud technology to ensure optimal resource utilization and cost efficiency.

See also: [Cloud Cost Optimization Standards](cloud_cost_optimization_standards.md) — the tagging, budgeting, and chargeback standards this role works within.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs cloud financial management frameworks and cost optimisation architectures ensuring cloud investments align with business strategy and deliver maximum value through governance, visibility, and accountability
- **Value Metrics:** Cloud cost reduction achieved, forecast accuracy, cloud resource utilisation rate, committed capacity optimisation percentage, unit economics improvements, FinOps maturity score
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, CIO, CFO/Financial Controllers, IT Directors, Cloud Platform Architects, business unit leaders
- **Processes Supported:** Cloud cost governance framework, cost allocation and tagging standards, chargeback/showback model design, FinOps tool evaluation and governance, cloud budget and forecast architecture, AI/LLM cost governance

## Key Responsibilities

- Design comprehensive cloud financial management frameworks that align with business objectives
- Establish cloud cost governance models, policies, and best practices
- Architect cost optimization solutions across multi-cloud environments
- Develop financial modeling and forecasting systems for cloud consumption including AI/LLM workloads
- Create standards for cost allocation, tagging, and chargeback/showback models
- Collaborate with cloud platform architects to ensure cost-efficient designs
- Govern AI/LLM API and compute costs (token usage, GPU reservation, spot/reserved capacity)
- Evaluate and recommend FinOps tools and technologies
- Lead the development of organizational FinOps maturity
- Drive sustainability and carbon footprint optimization alongside cloud cost initiatives
- Drive cloud cost optimization initiatives across business units

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Cloud financial management framework, cost governance standards, and FinOps tool selection | Cloud platform architecture decisions and their cost impact |
| Cost allocation model, chargeback/showback design, tagging taxonomy, and cost optimisation strategy | Business unit spending decisions and financial planning priorities |
| FinOps practice maturity roadmap and AI/LLM cost governance standards | Reserved capacity and commitment discount commercial negotiation decisions |

## Required Skills

- Deep understanding of cloud service provider pricing models and billing systems
- Expert knowledge of cloud architecture patterns and cost optimization techniques
- Experience with FinOps frameworks and methodologies
- Strong financial analysis and modeling capabilities
- Understanding of cost allocation and chargeback models
- Experience with cloud cost management tools and platforms
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
- Ability to translate technical concepts into business value

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Multi-cloud cost management platforms (CloudHealth, Apptio Cloudability, Spot by NetApp) for governance and optimisation architecture, Cloud financial governance frameworks (FinOps Foundation Framework v2, FOCUS cost allocation standard), Cloud-native cost tools (AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, GCP Billing & Recommender) for authoritative cost data, Showback/chargeback and cost allocation platform design including tagging taxonomy and allocation key definition
- **Proficient level required:** Cloud resource optimization platforms (Densify, Turbonomic, CAST AI) for rightsizing and workload placement architecture, Infrastructure as Code cost estimation (Infracost) for shift-left cost governance integration into CI/CD pipelines, AI/LLM cost governance (token attribution, GPU reservation vs. spot cost modelling, prompt caching optimisation)
- **Working Knowledge required:** Carbon footprint and sustainability reporting tools (Cloud Carbon Footprint, Azure Carbon Optimization, AWS Customer Carbon Footprint Tool, GCP Carbon Footprint), Enterprise architecture tooling (LeanIX, Sparx EA) for documenting cloud cost governance architecture
- **Awareness level expected:** FinOps automation and workflow orchestration platforms (Spot Ocean, CAST AI auto-scaling) for emerging optimisation patterns, Technology Business Management (TBM) frameworks and unit economics benchmarking standards

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; all FinOps design and governance work performed remotely through cloud billing portals and cost management platforms
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, cloud cost management platforms (CloudHealth, Cloudability, Azure Cost Management), and cloud provider billing portals
- **On-Site Requirements:** None routinely; occasional executive or stakeholder workshop attendance
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional cloud cost review forums and executive briefings
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call for operational issues; escalation path for significant cost anomalies, budget governance failures, or major cloud billing disputes
