## Role Overview

The Cloud Economics Analyst is the business-facing analytical capability within the FinOps practice, responsible for translating raw cloud spend data into actionable financial intelligence that informs budgeting, forecasting, and investment decisions. This role specialises in unit economics modelling, showback and chargeback reporting, budget variance analysis, TCO modelling, and make-vs-buy analysis, working closely with finance teams, product owners, and business unit leaders to ensure cloud costs are understood, attributed accurately, and optimised from a commercial perspective.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Team — cloud unit economics analysis, billing allocation accuracy, and commitment/contract data analysis
- **Experience Anchor:** 3-5 years in FinOps, financial analysis, or cloud engineering with a financial/economics focus — operates independently within the FinOps Senior Engineer's standards
- **Out of Scope:** FinOps architecture and cost governance standards (FinOps Architect-owned); billing pipeline engineering implementation (data engineering teams-owned, this role consumes it); tagging and rightsizing implementation (Cloud Cost Optimization Engineer-owned, this role analyses the resulting economics)
- **Escalates To:** FinOps Senior Engineer — cost economics questions
- **Escalated To By:** business unit finance leads on unit economics reporting needs

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Provides rigorous financial analysis and business-aligned reporting that enables leadership and business units to make informed decisions about cloud investment, cost accountability, and spend trajectory
- **Value Metrics:** Forecast accuracy percentage, chargeback report delivery SLA adherence, budget variance detection speed, unit cost trend analysis quality, stakeholder satisfaction score
- **Key Stakeholders:** FinOps Manager, Finance Director, business unit leaders, product owners, FinOps Senior Engineer, FinOps Architect, procurement teams
- **Processes Supported:** Monthly showback and chargeback reporting, cloud spend forecasting, budget variance analysis and escalation, TCO and make-vs-buy modelling, unit economics tracking, executive cost reporting and dashboards

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain showback and chargeback reports that accurately attribute cloud costs to business units, products, and cost centres
- Build and maintain cloud spend forecasting models, incorporating historical trends, committed spend, and pipeline growth signals
- Perform budget variance analysis, identify root causes of material deviations, and communicate findings to finance and business stakeholders
- Construct and maintain unit economics models (cost per transaction, cost per user, cost per API call) to support product and engineering decision-making
- Develop TCO models and make-vs-buy analyses to support cloud adoption, migration, and build-or-buy decisions
- Produce executive-level cloud cost dashboards and narrative reports for monthly and quarterly business reviews
- Partner with FinOps engineers to ensure cost allocation tagging accuracy underpins reliable reporting outputs
- Validate billing data accuracy across AWS, Azure, and GCP, identifying and resolving discrepancies with cloud providers or internal teams
- Support annual cloud budgeting cycles with historical analysis, trend modelling, and scenario planning inputs
- Continuously improve reporting automation, data pipelines, and dashboard quality to reduce manual effort and increase analytical depth

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Strong analytical and financial modelling skills, including forecasting, variance analysis, and TCO construction
- Proficiency in data visualisation tools such as Power BI or Tableau for building stakeholder-ready dashboards and reports
- Advanced Excel skills including financial modelling, pivot tables, and scenario analysis
- Working knowledge of cloud billing data structures across AWS (CUR), Azure (Cost Management exports), and GCP (BigQuery Billing Export)
- Ability to write SQL queries for billing data extraction, transformation, and aggregation
- Familiarity with Python for data analysis, automation of reporting pipelines, and integration with cloud billing APIs
- Clear and compelling communication skills, with the ability to translate complex cost data into business-relevant insights for non-technical audiences
- Understanding of FinOps principles, cost allocation strategies, and chargeback/showback methodologies
- Strong attention to detail and data quality discipline in financial reporting contexts

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Refreshing and validating monthly chargeback and showback reports for business unit distribution
- Updating cloud spend forecasting models with latest actuals and adjusting forward projections
- Investigating budget variance alerts and preparing concise written explanations for finance stakeholders
- Building or refining Power BI or Tableau dashboards in response to stakeholder requests
- Querying AWS CUR, Azure billing exports, or GCP BigQuery billing data to answer ad hoc cost questions
- Collaborating with FinOps engineers to validate that tagging coverage and allocation accuracy meet reporting requirements
- Preparing slides and talking points for monthly or quarterly cloud cost business reviews
- Running TCO or make-vs-buy analysis in support of ongoing migration or build decisions
- Documenting forecast assumptions, methodology changes, and data quality issues in the team knowledge base
- Attending finance and FinOps team meetings to align on budgeting timelines, reporting requirements, and cost reduction targets

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; all analytical, modelling, reporting, and dashboard development work is performed through cloud billing platforms, BI tools, and collaborative productivity tools
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, Jira, SharePoint, and cloud billing platforms including AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, and GCP BigQuery
- **On-Site Requirements:** None; occasional in-person attendance at quarterly business reviews or planning workshops may be requested but is not mandatory
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Core business hours aligned to primary finance and business unit stakeholder locations; flexibility required for cross-region cloud billing data processing cycles
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** No on-call requirement under normal operations; responsiveness expected during month-end close periods and quarterly budget cycles for urgent variance or forecast queries
