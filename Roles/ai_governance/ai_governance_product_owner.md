## Role Overview

The AI Governance Product Owner owns the AI governance product backlog, managing the roadmap for governance platform capabilities and tooling investments. This role bridges business risk and compliance requirements with technical governance implementation — working closely with the AI Governance Architect to translate governance policies into platform features, and with Legal, Compliance, and Risk Management to ensure the governance programme meets organisational and regulatory obligations. The AI Governance Product Owner ensures the governance team delivers prioritised, high-value capabilities that keep the organisation's AI estate compliant, auditable, and responsibly governed.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — AI governance programme backlog, risk register prioritisation, and delivery roadmap
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management, ideally with risk/compliance domain exposure — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** AI governance framework design (AI Governance Architect-owned); AI platform delivery roadmap (AI Platform Architect-owned, this role aligns to it); enterprise risk appetite setting (Risk Management-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Data & AI Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** AI Governance Senior Engineers and Engineers on backlog ceremonies and delivery prioritisation

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Maximise the effectiveness of the organisation's AI governance investment by ensuring the governance programme backlog is prioritised to address the highest-risk regulatory and compliance gaps, deliver measurable audit readiness, and provide tooling that enables AI teams to govern models without impeding delivery velocity.
- **Value Metrics:** Regulatory readiness score (EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF), AI governance backlog delivery rate, time-to-compliance for new regulatory requirements, AI governance tooling adoption rate by AI teams, audit finding rate trend.
- **Key Stakeholders:** AI Governance Architect, Chief AI Officer / Chief Risk Officer, Legal and Compliance, Risk Management, Business Unit AI programme leads, Internal Audit.
- **Processes Supported:** AI governance roadmap planning, sprint ceremonies and backlog management, regulatory change impact assessment and prioritisation, AI governance tooling procurement, AI compliance reporting and audit readiness coordination.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and manage the AI governance product backlog: create, groom, and prioritise user stories and governance capability epics with clear acceptance criteria aligned to regulatory and risk priorities.
- Develop and maintain the AI governance platform roadmap aligned to regulatory milestones (EU AI Act compliance timeline, ISO/IEC 42001 certification programme) and business risk appetite.
- Translate regulatory requirements (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, GDPR-AI intersection) and internal risk policies into actionable governance platform features and process improvements.
- Work with the AI Governance Architect to sequence technical governance capability delivery: risk assessment tooling, bias evaluation pipeline integration, model registry governance hooks, and audit trail infrastructure.
- Engage Legal, Compliance, and Risk Management stakeholders to gather requirements and validate that governance deliverables satisfy regulatory and audit expectations.
- Manage relationships with Business Unit AI programme leads to balance governance requirements with development team productivity and delivery velocity.
- Define and track AI governance programme metrics: risk register coverage, documentation completeness, and audit readiness indicators.
- Lead agile ceremonies for the AI governance team: sprint planning, backlog refinement, sprint review, and retrospectives.
- Evaluate and support procurement of AI governance tooling (GRC platforms, responsible AI toolkits, AI safety platforms).
- Coordinate AI governance reporting to risk committees and board-level governance bodies, working with the Architect on content.
- Work with TAL (Technical Account Lead) and PAL (Platform Account Lead) on vendor governance tooling roadmaps and support agreements.

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Understanding of AI/ML concepts sufficient to communicate effectively with AI Governance Architects and ML engineering teams about governance tooling requirements.
- Familiarity with AI governance and regulatory frameworks: EU AI Act risk categories, NIST AI RMF core functions, ISO/IEC 42001 management system requirements.
- Experience with agile product ownership: backlog management, user story writing, acceptance criteria definition, and sprint ceremonies.
- Knowledge of GRC (governance, risk, and compliance) processes and tooling: risk registers, control libraries, compliance evidence management platforms.
- Understanding of responsible AI concepts: bias and fairness evaluation, explainability requirements, model documentation (model cards), and AI audit trail requirements.
- Ability to read and interpret regulatory text and translate obligations into platform feature requirements.
- Experience with product management and roadmapping tooling: Jira, Confluence, ProductBoard, or equivalent.

**Soft Skills & Leadership:**

- Skilled at navigating competing priorities between Legal, Compliance, Risk, and AI development teams — building consensus on governance programme priorities without creating adversarial dynamics.
- Strong stakeholder communication for executive reporting: ability to translate technical governance programme status into business risk language for risk committees and board audiences.
- Data-driven backlog prioritisation: uses risk posture metrics, regulatory deadlines, and audit findings to justify prioritisation decisions objectively.

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Jira/Confluence, ServiceNow GRC/Archer, Microsoft Purview
- **Proficient level required:** Power BI/Tableau, Azure DevOps/GitHub Projects, AI compliance platforms (OneTrust)
- **Working Knowledge required:** ProductBoard/Aha! (roadmap management), Microsoft Teams/SharePoint
- **Awareness level expected:** Azure AI Content Safety, Responsible AI Dashboard

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Reviewing and grooming the AI governance product backlog with the team.
- Running sprint planning, backlog refinement, and sprint review ceremonies.
- Meeting with Legal and Compliance stakeholders to gather and validate regulatory requirements.
- Translating new AI regulatory guidance into draft user stories and governance capability epics.
- Reviewing governance programme metrics dashboards and preparing status update reports for risk committees.
- Engaging with Business Unit AI programme leads to assess governance requirements for new AI initiatives.
- Working with the AI Governance Architect to review technical designs and ensure backlog items are correctly specified.
- Coordinating with vendors (TAL/PAL) on AI governance tooling roadmap updates and procurement timelines.
- Preparing sprint review outputs and governance programme dashboards for risk committee reporting.
- Assessing new AI regulatory developments and estimating impact on the governance programme backlog.

## Key Performance Indicators

- AI governance backlog delivery rate: ≥85% of committed sprint items delivered each sprint
- Regulatory milestone delivery: 100% of EU AI Act / ISO 42001 compliance programme milestones met on schedule
- AI risk register completeness: ≥95% of in-scope AI systems with assessments completed within the review cycle
- Audit readiness: zero critical audit findings attributable to governance programme delivery gaps in annual internal or external audits
- Governance tooling adoption: ≥80% of AI teams using governed processes (model cards, risk assessments) within 6 months of tooling launch
- Time-to-compliance: new regulatory requirements translated into backlog items within 10 business days of guidance publication
- Stakeholder satisfaction: AI governance programme NPS from Business Unit AI teams ≥7/10
