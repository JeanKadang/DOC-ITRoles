## Role Overview

The AI Governance Architect designs and governs the organisation's frameworks, policies, and technical controls for responsible and compliant use of artificial intelligence across all business units and AI system deployments. As AI and generative AI become deeply embedded in enterprise products, operations, and decision-making, this role ensures that AI systems are trustworthy, explainable, fair, secure, and compliant with applicable regulations (EU AI Act, US Executive Orders, GDPR). The AI Governance Architect bridges technical AI implementation with legal, ethical, and regulatory requirements.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Enable the organisation to adopt and scale AI capabilities confidently by ensuring AI systems are governed, risks are managed, and compliance with AI regulations is maintained - preserving trust with customers, regulators, and employees.
- **Value Metrics:** AI systems assessed and classified under risk framework, percentage of in-scope AI systems with documented risk and compliance records, AI incident rate and severity trend, regulatory readiness score for EU AI Act or equivalent, employee AI literacy completion rate.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, Chief Data Officer, Chief AI Officer (or equivalent), Legal and Compliance, Risk Management, HR, Product teams, Board-level governance.
- **Processes Supported:** AI system risk assessment, AI model lifecycle governance, AI audit and regulatory reporting, responsible AI use policy, AI incident response, AI supplier due diligence.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design the organisation's AI governance framework: risk classification taxonomy, assessment processes, controls library, and accountability structures aligned to EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO/IEC 42001.
- Define AI system inventory and risk categorisation methodology covering all AI applications in use - including third-party AI and GenAI services (OpenAI/Azure OpenAI, Copilot, SaaS AI features).
- Architect technical controls for responsible AI: model explainability standards, bias testing requirements, data provenance documentation, model versioning and audit trail design.
- Establish AI incident response framework: classification of AI-related harms, investigation procedures, and remediation governance.
- Define AI supplier assessment framework for third-party AI tool procurement due diligence.
- Design Microsoft 365 Copilot, Azure OpenAI, and GenAI deployment governance: data boundary controls, prompt logging, sensitive data exposure controls, and usage policies.
- Define AI model documentation standards: model cards, intended use documentation, known limitations, and performance metrics.
- Govern organisational AI use policies: acceptable use, prohibited use cases, employee guidance, and AI disclosure requirements.
- Implement EU AI Act compliance programs: classify AI systems by risk tier (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal), maintain conformity assessments for high-risk systems, and establish post-market monitoring.
- Provide architecture oversight for high-risk AI systems (automated decision making, HR AI, financial risk AI).
- Collaborate with the security team on AI-specific security threats: prompt injection, model inversion, adversarial attacks, and supply chain risks.
- Report to board-level governance committees on AI risk posture and regulatory readiness.

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Understanding of AI/ML lifecycle: training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and retraining.
- Knowledge of responsible/ethical AI concepts: fairness, explainability, transparency, accountability, bias detection.
- Familiarity with AI governance standards and frameworks: EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, OECD AI Principles.
- Experience with model explainability tools: SHAP, LIME, Microsoft InterpretML, or equivalent.
- Understanding of bias detection and fairness testing: Fairlearn, AI Fairness 360, demographic parity, equalised odds.
- Knowledge of generative AI risk considerations: hallucinations, prompt injection, data leakage, copyright risks.
- Familiarity with AI security frameworks: OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATLAS.
- Understanding of data governance requirements supporting AI: data lineage, consent management, GDPR data subject rights as applied to AI.
- Ability to define technical audit trail and logging requirements for AI system decisions.

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Ability to navigate complex intersections of legal, ethical, and technical domains.
- Executive-level communication for AI governance board reporting.
- Ability to influence AI product and development teams without creating adversarial dynamics.
- Policy writing skills for AI use policies and governance procedures.
- Facilitation of cross-functional AI ethics and governance committees.

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Azure AI Content Safety, Microsoft Purview, EU AI Act compliance tooling, SHAP/LIME/Microsoft InterpretML
- **Proficient level required:** Fairlearn/AI Fairness 360, MLflow, OWASP LLM Top 10/MITRE ATLAS, Presidio
- **Working Knowledge required:** Azure Machine Learning, Responsible AI Dashboard, ISO/IEC 42001
- **Awareness level expected:** NIST AI RMF, Microsoft Copilot Studio governance controls
