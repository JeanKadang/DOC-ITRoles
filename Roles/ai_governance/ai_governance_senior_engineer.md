## Role Overview

The AI Governance Senior Engineer implements, operates, and continuously improves the technical controls, tooling, and processes that make up the organization's AI governance framework. Working under the direction of the AI Governance Architect, this role translates governance policies into working technical implementations — running AI risk assessments, operating bias and explainability tooling, maintaining the AI system inventory, and ensuring that AI deployments comply with applicable regulations and internal policies. The Senior Engineer brings hands-on depth in AI/ML tooling combined with strong governance and compliance awareness.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — advanced AI risk assessments and governance tooling delivery within the AI Governance Architect's framework
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in AI/ML governance, risk, or compliance engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's framework
- **Out of Scope:** AI governance framework and policy design (Architect-owned); AI platform infrastructure (AI Platform Engineers-owned, this role embeds governance checks into it); regulatory legal interpretation (Legal and Compliance-owned)
- **Escalates To:** AI Governance Architect — framework, policy, and complex assessment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** AI Governance Engineers on day-to-day task direction and technical guidance

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures that AI governance controls are operationally effective and consistently applied, reducing the risk of harmful, non-compliant, or unexplainable AI outcomes across the organization's AI portfolio
- **Value Metrics:** AI system inventory completeness and currency, percentage of in-scope AI systems with completed risk assessments, mean time to complete governance reviews, audit finding remediation rate, bias testing coverage of production AI models
- **Key Stakeholders:** AI Governance Architect, AI Platform Architect, data science and ML engineering teams, Legal and Compliance, CISO, and business owners of AI systems
- **Processes Supported:** AI system risk assessment and classification, bias and fairness testing, model explainability reporting, AI supplier technical due diligence, AI incident investigation, GenAI deployment configuration review

## Key Responsibilities

- Operate the AI system inventory: onboard new AI systems, maintain classification records, and track assessment status
- Conduct AI risk assessments for new and existing AI systems, applying the organization's risk classification taxonomy
- Execute bias detection and fairness testing using approved tooling (Fairlearn, AI Fairness 360, or equivalent) and document findings
- Generate model explainability reports using SHAP, LIME, InterpretML, or equivalent tools for high-risk AI decisions
- Review and validate technical configurations of GenAI deployments (Azure OpenAI, Copilot, SaaS AI features) for data boundary and content filtering compliance
- Maintain the AI governance documentation library: model cards, risk assessment records, audit trails, and assessment reports
- Support AI supplier technical due diligence — reviewing vendor documentation, API security configurations, and data handling practices
- Investigate and document AI-related incidents using the AI incident response framework
- Monitor AI governance tooling health and maintain integrations with MLflow, Weights & Biases, or equivalent model registries
- Implement and maintain EU AI Act compliance controls: risk classification documentation, technical conformity assessment support, and post-market monitoring for high-risk AI systems
- Contribute to improving AI governance processes, assessment templates, and technical controls

- Familiarity with MLOps tooling: MLflow, Weights & Biases, Azure ML, or equivalent model registry and pipeline tools
- Understanding of responsible AI principles: fairness, explainability, transparency, and accountability
- Knowledge of GenAI platforms and their governance controls: Azure OpenAI content filtering, Microsoft Copilot admin controls, prompt logging
- Familiarity with AI regulatory frameworks: EU AI Act risk tiers, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 concepts
- Python proficiency for running bias tests, explainability analysis, and data processing scripts
- Strong documentation skills for producing governance records, model cards, and assessment reports

- Supporting supplier technical due diligence with questionnaire review and technical analysis
- Preparing governance evidence packs for internal audit or regulatory review cycles
- Contributing to improvements in assessment templates, tooling, and governance processes
