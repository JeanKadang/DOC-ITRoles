## Role Overview

The Enterprise Infrastructure Onboarding Senior Engineer leads the implementation of complex infrastructure provisioning and onboarding solutions that span multiple technology domains. This role provides technical leadership for creating standardized, automated, and efficient processes for deploying infrastructure resources.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads complex cross-platform infrastructure provisioning implementations, advanced automation frameworks, and multi-domain onboarding solutions ensuring rapid, reliable, and standardised infrastructure deployment at enterprise scale
- **Value Metrics:** Provisioning time improvements, automation framework coverage, provisioning success rate, incident resolution time, cross-domain standardisation rate
- **Key Stakeholders:** Infrastructure Onboarding Architect, domain senior engineers, DevOps Senior Engineers, Infrastructure Onboarding Product Owner
- **Processes Supported:** Complex multi-domain provisioning design, IaC automation development, governance controls implementation, onboarding standards development, engineer mentorship

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex infrastructure onboarding solutions across platforms
- Develop advanced automation frameworks for infrastructure provisioning
- Create technical specifications for multi-platform onboarding workflows
- Lead troubleshooting for critical onboarding and provisioning issues
- Establish standards for infrastructure deployment across technology domains
- Implement governance controls in provisioning processes
- Optimize infrastructure onboarding for speed and reliability
- Provide technical mentorship to Infrastructure Onboarding Engineers

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Complex provisioning implementation standards, IaC automation frameworks, and multi-platform onboarding workflows | Architecture design decisions and reference architecture governance |
| Service catalog technical implementation, provisioning governance controls, and automation quality standards | Product roadmap priorities and toolchain procurement decisions |
| Cross-domain provisioning integration patterns and senior engineering escalations | Data centre design and infrastructure lifecycle planning |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Extensive experience with infrastructure provisioning across multiple domains
- Advanced knowledge of infrastructure automation and orchestration
- Strong scripting and programming skills (PowerShell, Python, etc.)
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code frameworks
- Deep understanding of IT service management processes
- Knowledge of enterprise technology domains (compute, storage, network)
- Advanced troubleshooting skills for complex infrastructure issues
- Relevant certifications in multiple infrastructure domains

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Terraform/Ansible (infrastructure automation), ServiceNow (ITSM/service catalog), Azure DevOps/Jenkins (CI/CD)
- **Proficient level required:** Windows Server/Linux administration, Azure (IaaS), workflow automation platforms
- **Working Knowledge required:** API integration frameworks, infrastructure monitoring (Azure Monitor), CMDB management
- **Awareness level expected:** Kubernetes, infrastructure governance frameworks

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; all IaC development, automation, orchestration, and multi-domain provisioning managed remotely through infrastructure tooling and ITSM platforms
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, GitHub, Terraform, Ansible, ServiceNow, and Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions
- **On-Site Requirements:** None routinely; rare coordination with server hardware team for new infrastructure domain onboarding
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Core business hours with coverage for cross-team provisioning activities and sprint ceremonies
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** On-call for critical provisioning pipeline failures or major multi-domain onboarding failures affecting business operations
