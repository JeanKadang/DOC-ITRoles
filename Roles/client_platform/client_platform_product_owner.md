## Role Overview

The Client Platform Product Owner owns the backlog, roadmap, and delivery outcomes for the Client Platform domain, spanning the OS engineering practice, client hardware lifecycle, and application packaging pipeline across Windows 10/11, macOS, and Linux desktop (Ubuntu LTS). This role translates business requirements, security obligations, and workforce technology needs into a prioritised, well-defined backlog that the engineering team can execute against effectively.

The Product Owner acts as the primary business-facing point of contact for the Client Platform team, managing stakeholder relationships with HR (onboarding), Procurement (hardware lifecycle and device refresh investment), Security (compliance and hardening obligations), and the Service Desk. As the primary hardware vendor relationship manager, this role maintains the commercial partnership with Lenovo — the organisation's principal hardware OEM for ThinkPad laptops and ThinkCentre desktops — alongside Apple for the MacBook Pro/Air fleet.

They define acceptance criteria for OS releases and application packages, govern the hardware refresh programme, and provide transparent reporting on client estate health, OS currency, and refresh status to IT leadership. This role does not require deep hands-on OS engineering capability, but does require sufficient technical literacy to articulate requirements clearly, critically evaluate engineering effort estimates, and make prioritisation decisions with confidence.

The Product Owner is accountable for ensuring the team delivers measurable outcomes aligned to business value, not just engineering outputs.

- **Business Objective:** Maximise workforce productivity and minimise risk by ensuring the client OS estate is modern, secure, and well-maintained — with predictable delivery of OS releases, device provisioning, and hardware refresh cycles that meet business and regulatory expectations.
- **Value Metrics:** Device provisioning SLA adherence for new starters, OS currency rate (% of fleet on current approved OS version), hardware refresh completion against plan, application packaging backlog lead time, client estate patch compliance rate, security hardening compliance score.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CIO/CTO, CISO, IT Operations leadership, HR/People Operations, Procurement, Finance, Service Desk, Security Architecture, Endpoint Management Product Owner.
- **Processes Supported:** New employee device provisioning, hardware refresh planning and budgeting, OS lifecycle management, application onboarding backlog, client estate compliance reporting, vendor and OEM relationship management.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — client platform backlog, roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Engineering solution design and implementation approach (Architect and engineers-owned); OS hardening and security baseline requirements (Security/Architect-owned); hardware lifecycle standards and OEM selection (Architect/Procurement-owned); budget envelope and headcount (Chapter Lead-owned)
- **Escalates To:** End User & Workplace Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Client Platform Senior Engineer on backlog refinement and delivery-transparency questions

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensure the client platform service delivers maximum workforce value — a secure, reliable, and modern device experience — by prioritising the engineering backlog around business outcomes, stakeholder needs, and platform health.
- **Value Metrics:** Stakeholder satisfaction with the client platform service, backlog lead time for prioritised platform features, provisioning and packaging service-level performance, adoption rate of new platform capabilities (e.g., zero-touch provisioning), platform compliance and patch currency trends.
- **Key Stakeholders:** End User & Workplace Chapter Lead, Client Platform Architect and engineering team, Endpoint Management Product Owner, Service Desk Lead, HR (onboarding experience), Procurement, Security, and business unit representatives.
- **Processes Supported:** Client platform backlog and roadmap management, demand intake and prioritisation, new capability rollout planning, stakeholder communication, and service performance review.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and maintain the Client Platform product backlog, ensuring stories are well-defined, estimated, and prioritised in alignment with business value and technical risk.
- Define acceptance criteria for OS image releases, application package deployments, and provisioning pipeline changes, working closely with the Senior Engineer to ensure definitions of done are clear and testable.
- Lead hardware refresh programme planning: define device lifecycle policies for the Lenovo ThinkPad (T/X/L/E series), ThinkCentre desktop, and MacBook fleet; coordinate with Procurement on Lenovo refresh schedules and budget submissions; track delivery and Lenovo Premier Support warranty coverage against plan.
- Manage the OS and client tooling roadmap, incorporating vendor release schedules (Windows 11 feature updates, macOS annual releases, Ubuntu LTS upgrade cycles), security requirements, and workforce technology changes.
- Serve as the liaison between Client Platform and key stakeholder groups: HR for onboarding device readiness SLAs, Procurement for OEM selection and hardware standards governance, Security for hardening and compliance obligations.
- Report on client estate health metrics to IT leadership: OS compliance rate, hardware refresh status, patch currency, and provisioning SLA performance.
- Manage vendor relationships with Microsoft, Apple, and Lenovo as the primary hardware OEM, including Lenovo Premier Support contract governance, hardware roadmap briefings (ThinkPad and ThinkCentre product lines), and commercial escalation management; maintain secondary relationships with other OEMs where applicable.
- Define and maintain the application packaging intake process, triage inbound packaging requests by business priority, and manage expectations with requesting teams.
- Coordinate with the Endpoint Management Product Owner to ensure aligned priorities where Client Platform engineering intersects with UEM platform capabilities.
- Ensure hardware asset lifecycle data for the Lenovo and Apple fleet is accurately maintained in the ITAM system (ServiceNow ITAM or equivalent), aligned with Lenovo Premier Support warranty records and enabling accurate refresh cycle forecasting.
- Remove blockers for the engineering team through stakeholder escalation, procurement facilitation, and dependency resolution.

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Sufficient understanding of OS engineering concepts across Windows 10/11, macOS, and Linux desktop to evaluate engineering complexity, ask informed questions, and assess risk in backlog items.
- Familiarity with client-side patch management concepts: Windows Update for Business, Jamf Software Update, and Linux package management — to understand patching cadences and compliance reporting.
- Working knowledge of application packaging concepts (MSIX, .pkg, .deb) to triage and prioritise packaging intake requests effectively.
- Ability to read and interpret client estate health dashboards: OS compliance reports, Intune Endpoint Analytics, Jamf Pro dashboards, and Windows Update for Business Reports.
- Understanding of hardware lifecycle management: device refresh cycles for a Lenovo-primary fleet (ThinkPad T/X/L/E series, ThinkCentre) and Apple MacBook estate, Lenovo commercial procurement processes, Lenovo Premier Support warranty governance, and ITAM system integration for lifecycle data accuracy.
- Familiarity with agile delivery practices: backlog management, sprint planning, story mapping, and velocity tracking in tools such as Jira or Azure DevOps.
- Experience producing stakeholder reporting and executive-level dashboards using tools such as Power BI, SharePoint, or similar.

- Empathy for end-user experience, balancing security and operational requirements against workforce productivity.
- Ability to facilitate cross-functional workshops and planning sessions involving Security, HR, Procurement, and engineering.
- Organised and data-driven, with a commitment to transparent, metrics-based reporting on team delivery and estate health.

## Common Challenges

- **Competing stakeholder priorities:** Balancing urgent security hardening obligations (CISO), new starter provisioning SLAs (HR), and hardware refresh programme delivery (Procurement and Finance) against a finite engineering team capacity.
- **OS vendor release cadence pressure:** macOS annual releases, Windows feature updates, and Ubuntu LTS bi-annual cycles create recurring pipeline demand that must be planned into the roadmap without crowding out other backlog items.
- **Hardware refresh budget cycles:** Aligning Lenovo ThinkPad and MacBook lifecycle refresh plans with annual CapEx budget rounds requires forward planning 12–18 months ahead, incorporating Lenovo Premier Support warranty expiry data, device age and failure rate trends, and Lenovo product generation roadmaps — data that is often incomplete or not yet consolidated in the ITAM system.
- **Scope boundary management:** Clearly and consistently maintaining the boundary between Client Platform (OS engineering) and Endpoint Management (UEM policy/MDM) with stakeholders who do not always distinguish between the two domains.
- **Application packaging backlog demand:** Inbound packaging requests from business teams can arrive ad hoc and with inflated urgency claims, requiring a disciplined intake and prioritisation process backed by stakeholder agreements.
