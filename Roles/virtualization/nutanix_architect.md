## Role Overview

The Nutanix Architect designs and oversees the organization's hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) based on Nutanix technology. This role ensures the delivery of a scalable, efficient, and resilient platform that meets business requirements for application hosting and services.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure architecture and technology standards across the chapter
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in virtualisation or hyperconverged infrastructure architecture with demonstrated Nutanix ownership — operates independently on domain-wide Nutanix architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** VMware or Hyper-V virtualisation architecture (VMware/Hyper-V Architects-owned — parallel, non-Nutanix virtualisation ladders); server hardware specification (Server Hardware Architect-owned, this role consumes it); database workload tuning detail (Database Architect-owned, this role optimises Nutanix for it)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities and cross-domain investment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** Nutanix Senior Engineers on solution design and implementation strategy

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs Nutanix HCI architecture enabling infrastructure consolidation, software-defined compute/storage/networking, and cloud-like agility for enterprise workload hosting
- **Value Metrics:** Infrastructure consolidation ratio, VM density, HCI total cost of ownership, platform availability, cloud integration readiness, hardware refresh efficiency
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, application teams, storage and network architects, server hardware teams, cloud platform architects, IT leadership
- **Processes Supported:** HCI platform standards, VM lifecycle management, HA/DR design, hybrid cloud integration with Nutanix Clusters, capacity planning

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and architect Nutanix-based hyperconverged infrastructure solutions
- Develop HCI standards, policies, and best practices for Nutanix deployments
- Create designs for compute, storage, and network integration
- Plan and architect Nutanix upgrades, migrations, and expansions
- Optimize Nutanix platform performance and resource utilization
- Design high availability and disaster recovery solutions using Nutanix technologies
- Develop automation strategies leveraging Nutanix APIs and tools
- Provide technical leadership for Nutanix-related initiatives

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep knowledge of Nutanix products and technologies (AHV, Prism, Files, Flow, etc.)
- Extensive experience with hyperconverged infrastructure design
- Understanding of software-defined storage and networking concepts
- Knowledge of virtualization technologies and container platforms
- Experience with Nutanix performance optimization and capacity planning
- Familiarity with infrastructure automation tools
- Understanding of high availability and disaster recovery concepts
- Relevant certifications (Nutanix Certified Expert, Nutanix Platform Professional)

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates complex HCI design proposals and tradeoffs clearly to server hardware teams, storage architects, and IT leadership, enabling informed infrastructure investment decisions
- Builds strong cross-functional relationships with database, Kubernetes, cloud, and backup teams to ensure Nutanix architecture meets diverse workload requirements
- Applies systematic analytical thinking to evaluate HCI design options, sizing decisions, and capacity models, balancing technical performance requirements with cost efficiency and operational simplicity

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Nutanix AOS and AHV hypervisor architecture, cluster design, and sizing methodology, Nutanix Prism Central for enterprise multi-cluster governance and policy automation, Nutanix Flow microsegmentation and network security architecture, Nutanix HCI disaster recovery design (Nutanix Leap, Metro Availability, Async DR)
- **Proficient level required:** Nutanix Calm for application lifecycle automation and blueprint design, Nutanix Files and Objects software-defined storage architecture and design, Nutanix Era for database-as-a-service workload placement, Nutanix Karbon for Kubernetes platform design and integration
- **Working Knowledge required:** Nutanix Clusters and Xi for hybrid and multi-cloud workload placement strategy, Nutanix Beam for multi-cloud cost governance and chargeback, Nutanix Foundation for automated large-scale cluster deployment
- **Awareness level expected:** Nutanix Frame for desktop-as-a-service product capabilities, Cloud-native HCI competitive landscape (VMware vSAN, Azure Stack HCI, HPE SimpliVity)
