## Role Overview

The Nutanix Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of complex Nutanix hyperconverged solutions. This role provides technical leadership for Nutanix deployments, migrations, and operations while working closely with architects to translate HCI designs into effective implementations.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — advanced Nutanix solution design and delivery within the Nutanix Architect's reference architecture
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in Nutanix or hyperconverged infrastructure engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's reference architecture
- **Out of Scope:** Nutanix platform architecture and standards (Architect-owned); database workload optimisation ownership (Database Senior Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it); hybrid cloud integration ownership (Cloud Senior Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it)
- **Escalates To:** Nutanix Architect — solution design and implementation strategy exceptions
- **Escalated To By:** Nutanix Engineers on technical implementation issues

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads complex Nutanix HCI implementations and optimizations ensuring high-performance, resilient hyperconverged infrastructure reliably supporting enterprise application workloads
- **Value Metrics:** HCI platform availability, automation coverage for Nutanix operations, resource utilization efficiency, upgrade and migration success rates, incident resolution time
- **Key Stakeholders:** Nutanix Architect, application teams, database teams, security engineers, cloud teams
- **Processes Supported:** Complex HCI design and implementation, Nutanix automation and scripting, HA/DR delivery, cluster management, performance tuning and capacity optimization

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex Nutanix solutions based on architectural guidance
- Develop automation and orchestration for hyperconverged infrastructure management
- Lead Nutanix cluster design and implementation for high availability
- Create and maintain Nutanix infrastructure standards and procedures
- Lead troubleshooting for critical hyperconverged infrastructure issues
- Implement advanced storage and networking configurations
- Optimize resource allocation and infrastructure performance
- Provide technical mentorship to Nutanix Engineers

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; Nutanix platform management is primarily performed through Prism Central, APIs, and remote tooling
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Nutanix Prism Central, PowerShell/REST API automation tools, and monitoring platforms
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site access for physical hardware tasks such as node replacement or new cluster rack-and-stack
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Core business hours with flexibility for maintenance windows and cluster upgrade activities
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** On-call for critical Nutanix cluster failures, node outages, and major HCI incidents requiring advanced technical intervention
