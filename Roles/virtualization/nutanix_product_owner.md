## Role Overview

The Nutanix Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) solutions. This role leads a team of Nutanix architects and engineers, ensuring that hyperconverged services meet business requirements, performance targets, and operational standards.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the Nutanix HCI platform delivers infrastructure efficiency and cost-effectiveness enabling reliable, scalable delivery of enterprise application workloads on hyperconverged infrastructure
- **Value Metrics:** HCI total cost of ownership, platform availability, resource utilization, time-to-provision for infrastructure requests, licensing and hardware cost optimization
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, IT leadership, application product owners, procurement, finance, server hardware teams
- **Processes Supported:** Nutanix platform backlog and roadmap management, capacity planning, hardware refresh coordination, licensing governance, agile delivery ceremonies

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for Nutanix infrastructure and services
- Prioritize hyperconverged initiatives based on business impact and strategic alignment
- Define acceptance criteria for Nutanix platform deliverables
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the Nutanix team
- Engage with business stakeholders to understand HCI requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for Nutanix platform evolution
- Manage capacity planning and resource allocation for hyperconverged resources
- Track and report on Nutanix platform metrics and service levels

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

| Owns | Advises On |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Strong understanding of Nutanix technologies and hyperconverged infrastructure
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product ownership
- Background in infrastructure architecture or engineering
- Knowledge of IT service management principles
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication abilities
- Strategic thinking and planning capabilities
- Experience with technology roadmap development
- Understanding of infrastructure licensing and cost models

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Nutanix HCI platform capabilities, licensing models, and strategic roadmap management, Jira and Confluence for agile backlog management, sprint planning, and stakeholder communication, Nutanix Prism Central product features and service catalog governance, Nutanix node licensing and NX/third-party hardware cost management
- **Proficient level required:** Nutanix Beam for multi-cloud cost governance and utilisation chargeback reporting, Nutanix AOS upgrade and lifecycle management at programme planning level, Capacity planning and resource utilisation dashboards in Prism Central, Stakeholder-facing reporting and HCI service KPI tracking tools
- **Working Knowledge required:** Nutanix Calm and automation capabilities for self-service provisioning design, Nutanix Flow microsegmentation and security policy management at governance level, Nutanix Clusters on public clouds for hybrid workload placement decisions
- **Awareness level expected:** Nutanix Frame desktop-as-a-service capabilities and competitive positioning, Competitive HCI landscape (VMware vSAN, Azure Stack HCI, HPE SimpliVity) for market context

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; product ownership is stakeholder engagement, backlog management, and roadmap planning
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, Nutanix Beam dashboards, and reporting tools
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for executive stakeholder sessions, significant hardware refresh planning reviews, or vendor QBRs with Nutanix

- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call; available through escalation for critical Nutanix HCI failures requiring executive communication or emergency procurement decisions
