## Role Overview

The VMware Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of complex VMware virtualization solutions. This role provides technical leadership for virtualization deployments, migrations, and operations while working closely with architects to translate virtualization designs into effective implementations.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — advanced VMware solution design and delivery within the VMware Architect's reference architecture
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in VMware engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's reference architecture
- **Out of Scope:** VMware platform architecture and standards (Architect-owned); advanced storage virtualisation ownership (Storage Senior Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it); complex virtual networking design (Network Senior Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it)
- **Escalates To:** VMware Architect — solution design and implementation strategy exceptions
- **Escalated To By:** VMware Engineers on technical implementation issues

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads complex VMware platform implementations and optimizations ensuring high-performance, resilient virtualization infrastructure reliably supporting enterprise application workloads
- **Value Metrics:** Infrastructure availability, VM deployment automation coverage, resource utilization efficiency, migration success rates, incident resolution time for critical virtualization issues
- **Key Stakeholders:** VMware Architect, storage teams, network teams, application teams, security engineers
- **Processes Supported:** Complex vSphere design and implementation, PowerCLI automation, NSX-T advanced configuration, vSAN optimization, HA/DR delivery, capacity planning

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex VMware solutions based on architectural guidance
- Develop automation and orchestration for virtualization management
- Lead vSphere implementation for high-availability and fault tolerance
- Create and maintain virtualization standards and procedures
- Lead troubleshooting for critical virtualization issues
- Implement advanced storage and network virtualization
- Optimize resource allocation and virtual infrastructure performance
- Provide technical mentorship to VMware Engineers

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| VMware solution implementation approach, technical standards enforcement, and advanced vSphere configuration | VMware architecture direction and strategic platform decisions |
| PowerCLI automation frameworks, vCenter scripting standards, and operational efficiency tooling | VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) strategy and long-term product roadmap |
| VMware performance tuning, vSAN optimization, and complex incident resolution | NSX-T advanced network design and DR/HA architecture decisions |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Extensive experience with VMware technologies and enterprise virtualization
- Advanced knowledge of vSphere design and implementation
- Strong understanding of virtual storage and networking solutions
- Experience with PowerCLI and virtualization automation
- Deep knowledge of virtualization performance tuning and optimization
- Experience with VMware disaster recovery and business continuity solutions
- Advanced troubleshooting skills for complex virtualization environments
- Relevant advanced certifications (VCAP, vExpert)

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates complex VMware technical findings, designs, and recommendations clearly to architects, product owners, and operations staff; documents complex configurations with precision
- Leads cross-functional collaboration with storage, network, cloud, and security teams on complex VMware projects; mentors VMware Engineers on advanced vSphere, vSAN, and NSX techniques
- Applies systematic diagnostic approaches to complex virtualization incidents; proactively identifies platform optimization and automation opportunities that improve infrastructure reliability and efficiency

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** VMware vSphere (ESXi 7.0/8.0, vCenter Server) at advanced configuration, cluster design, and performance optimisation level, VMware vSAN storage design, deduplication, compression, and performance tuning, VMware NSX-T software-defined networking and microsegmentation implementation, PowerCLI scripting and automation for vSphere operational management and bulk tasks
- **Proficient level required:** VMware Site Recovery Manager (SRM) and vSphere Replication for DR implementation and failover testing, VMware vRealize Operations (Aria Operations) and Log Insight for performance management, VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) lifecycle management and SDDC upgrade procedures, VMware Aria Suite for infrastructure automation and orchestration
- **Working Knowledge required:** VMware Tanzu for Kubernetes workload management and integration, VMware HCX for workload mobility and cloud migration workflows, vRealize Orchestrator for complex multi-step workflow automation
- **Awareness level expected:** Broadcom VMware product consolidation and licensing changes impact on platform strategy, Emerging cloud-native alternatives and Azure VMware Solution integration patterns
