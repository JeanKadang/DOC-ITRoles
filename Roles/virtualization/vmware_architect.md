## Role Overview

The VMware Architect designs and oversees the organization's virtualization infrastructure based on VMware technologies. This role ensures the delivery of a scalable, performant, and reliable virtualization platform supporting enterprise applications and services.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — VMware virtualisation platform architecture and technology standards across the chapter
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in virtualisation architecture with demonstrated architecture-level delivery — operates independently on domain-wide VMware architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Nutanix or Hyper-V virtualisation architecture (Nutanix/Hyper-V Architects-owned — parallel, non-VMware virtualisation ladders); server hardware specification (Server Hardware Architect-owned, this role consumes it); virtual storage detail implementation (Storage Architects-owned, this role coordinates with it)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities and cross-domain investment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** VMware Senior Engineers on solution design and implementation strategy

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs the enterprise VMware virtualization platform enabling infrastructure consolidation, SDDC capabilities, and reliable workload hosting through vSphere, vSAN, and NSX technologies
- **Value Metrics:** VM consolidation ratio, infrastructure cost reduction, platform availability, vSAN storage efficiency, NSX microsegmentation coverage, workload migration success rate
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, server hardware architects, storage architects, network architects, security architects, cloud platform architects
- **Processes Supported:** VMware platform standards governance, vSphere cluster design, NSX network virtualization, DR/HA architecture, hybrid cloud integration

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and architect VMware solutions including vSphere, vSAN, NSX, and vRealize
- Develop virtualization standards, policies, and best practices
- Create designs for compute, storage, and network virtualization components
- Plan and architect VMware upgrades, migrations, and transformations
- Optimize VMware platform performance and resource utilization
- Design high availability and disaster recovery solutions using VMware technologies
- Develop automation and orchestration strategies for VMware environments
- Provide technical leadership for virtualization initiatives

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep knowledge of VMware products and technologies (vSphere, vSAN, NSX, vRealize)
- Extensive experience with enterprise virtualization design and architecture
- Understanding of software-defined data center concepts
- Knowledge of virtual networking and storage technologies
- Experience with virtualization performance optimization
- Familiarity with infrastructure automation tools
- Understanding of high availability and disaster recovery concepts
- Relevant certifications (VMware Certified Design Expert, VMware Certified Advanced Professional)

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates complex VMware SDDC architectural designs and tradeoffs clearly to IT leadership, infrastructure teams, and application stakeholders to drive informed platform investment decisions
- Builds strong cross-functional relationships with storage, network, security, and cloud teams to ensure cohesive, integrated virtualization architecture across the enterprise
- Applies structured analytical thinking to evaluate VMware technology choices, version strategies, and SDDC evolution, balancing performance, resilience, licensing cost, and operational complexity

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

**Expert level required:**

- VMware vSphere (ESXi, vCenter Server) architecture and SDDC design patterns
- VMware NSX-T software-defined networking and microsegmentation architecture
- VMware vSAN storage design, performance optimisation, and policy management
- VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) platform architecture and lifecycle management

**Proficient level required:**

- VMware vRealize Suite and Aria Suite for automation, operations, and lifecycle management
- VMware Site Recovery Manager (SRM) and vSphere Replication for DR architecture
- VMware Horizon VDI platform architecture and design
- VMware HCX for workload mobility and cloud migration

**Working Knowledge required:**

- VMware Tanzu for Kubernetes and container workload integration
- PowerCLI and vRealize Orchestrator for automation strategy and scripting
- VMware Carbon Black for virtualisation security controls

**Awareness level expected:**

- Broadcom VMware licensing model changes and platform consolidation roadmap
- Azure VMware Solution and VMware Cloud on AWS for hybrid cloud strategy
