## Role Overview

The Hyper-V Product Owner manages the lifecycle and roadmap of Microsoft virtualization platforms within the organization. This role balances business requirements with technical capabilities to ensure Hyper-V environments deliver value to stakeholders while maintaining operational excellence.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the Hyper-V platform maximizes virtualization ROI and infrastructure efficiency, delivering reliable virtual services that support business applications on the Microsoft Windows ecosystem
- **Value Metrics:** Platform availability, VM density and consolidation ratio, infrastructure cost reduction, roadmap delivery completion, stakeholder satisfaction, Windows licensing cost optimization
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, IT leadership, application product owners, Windows Server teams, procurement, finance
- **Processes Supported:** VM lifecycle management, platform capacity planning, hardware refresh planning, service catalog management, agile delivery ceremonies

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage the product backlog for Hyper-V infrastructure services
- Prioritize initiatives and features based on business impact and technical requirements
- Define acceptance criteria and service level objectives for Hyper-V environments
- Lead agile ceremonies and sprint planning for virtualization teams
- Engage with stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into technical implementations
- Create and maintain the strategic roadmap for Hyper-V platforms
- Track and report on key metrics for Hyper-V service delivery
- Manage the lifecycle of Hyper-V technologies, including upgrade paths and version transitions
- Balance innovation and stability in the Hyper-V product portfolio
- Coordinate with Windows licensing teams for compliance and cost optimization
- Manage dependencies with other technology domains (storage, networking, security)
- Advocate for customer needs in virtualization service delivery

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Hyper-V platform product backlog prioritization and strategic roadmap | Hyper-V architecture decisions and Azure Stack HCI investment strategy |
| Acceptance criteria and delivery scope for Hyper-V platform capabilities and service catalog | Windows Server and cloud platform strategy, Azure integration roadmap |
| Windows licensing governance, cost optimization approach, and vendor relationship management | Storage, network, and security platform investment decisions affecting virtualization |

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Strong understanding of Microsoft Hyper-V technologies and capabilities
- Experience with product management methodologies in technical environments
- Knowledge of agile frameworks and backlog management
- Familiarity with Windows Server licensing models and cost structures
- Understanding of virtualization concepts and market trends
- Ability to translate business requirements into technical specifications
- Experience with service management and IT operations
- Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
- Strategic thinking and roadmap development experience
- Data-driven decision making and metrics analysis capabilities
- Understanding of IT financial management and TCO considerations

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Translates complex Hyper-V platform capabilities into clear business value statements for IT leadership and stakeholders; presents platform roadmaps and metrics effectively across technical and non-technical audiences
- Builds strong collaborative partnerships with engineering teams, Windows Server teams, procurement, and finance to align platform delivery with business needs and budget constraints
- Balances technical depth with pragmatic prioritization, making evidence-based backlog decisions using platform usage data, stakeholder feedback, reliability trends, and total cost of ownership analysis

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Microsoft Hyper-V platform capabilities and Windows Server licensing models for product governance, Jira and Confluence for agile backlog management, sprint planning, and roadmap communication, Azure Stack HCI platform roadmap and infrastructure investment strategy, Windows Server Datacenter and Standard licensing governance and cost optimisation
- **Proficient level required:** System Center Virtual Machine Manager (SCVMM) product capabilities and service catalog design, Windows Admin Center for platform health monitoring and service reporting, Azure hybrid integration features and virtual machine migration tooling, Capacity planning and infrastructure utilisation reporting
- **Working Knowledge required:** Hyper-V Replica and Azure Site Recovery for DR planning and SLA governance, Software-Defined Networking product capabilities in Windows Server, PowerShell automation and self-service provisioning pattern design
- **Awareness level expected:** Azure Arc and Azure Stack HCI platform convergence roadmap, VMware and Nutanix competitive positioning for infrastructure strategy context
