## Role Overview

The Security Cross-Platform Product Owner manages the portfolio of security services and capabilities that span multiple technology domains. This role ensures that security requirements are prioritized and implemented consistently across all platforms, balancing security needs with operational efficiency and user experience.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — cross-platform security programme backlog and roadmap alignment across domain-specific product roadmaps
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management, ideally with security domain exposure — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Cross-platform security architecture (Security Cross-Platform Architect-owned); domain-specific product roadmap ownership (domain-specific Product Owners-owned, this role aligns cross-cutting priorities with them); regulatory compliance interpretation (security compliance teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** project managers on cross-platform security implementations

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the organisation's cross-platform security investment delivers consistent risk reduction across the entire technology estate by managing a unified security capability roadmap that coordinates security initiatives across all domain teams rather than leaving gaps at platform boundaries.
- **Value Metrics:** Cross-platform security control consistency score, security initiative delivery rate across domain teams, risk reduction trend across multi-platform estate, compliance achievement across heterogeneous environments, stakeholder satisfaction with cross-platform security services.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, domain product owners (Windows, Linux, Cloud, Kubernetes), Security Cross-Platform Architect, Legal and Compliance, Risk Management, Enterprise Architecture.
- **Processes Supported:** Cross-platform security roadmap management, multi-domain sprint coordination, regulatory compliance programme, security awareness across platform teams, cross-domain vulnerability prioritisation.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain a product roadmap for cross-platform security capabilities
- Prioritize security initiatives across multiple technology domains based on risk and business impact
- Create and refine user stories for security features that will be implemented across different platforms
- Manage backlogs for cross-platform security projects and coordinate with domain-specific teams
- Establish metrics and KPIs to measure the effectiveness of security controls across various platforms
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand security requirements that span multiple domains
- Facilitate agile ceremonies for cross-platform security initiatives
- Balance security requirements with operational needs and technology constraints across domains
