## Role Overview

The ITSM Product Owner owns the product vision, roadmap, and backlog for the organisation's IT Service Management platform and Configuration Management Database (CMDB). This role ensures the ITSM platform (typically ServiceNow or equivalent) evolves to meet the changing needs of IT Operations, end users, and the business - covering Incident, Problem, Change, Service Request, Asset, CMDB, and related ITSM processes. The PO balances platform capability development, process improvement, and stakeholder expectations across IT and business domains.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — ITSM platform backlog, roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** ITIL process design (with Process Owners/Service Management Architect); ITSM tooling procurement commercial decisions (with Procurement); organisational ITSM process adoption strategy; IT security policies applicable to the ITSM platform
- **Escalates To:** Service & Governance Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** ITSM / ConfigMgr Engineers on backlog clarification and acceptance criteria questions

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Continuously improve ITSM platform capabilities to enhance service quality, reduce operational friction, and provide accurate IT data to support governance and business decisions.
- **Value Metrics:** Incident resolution time trend, first-call resolution rate, change success rate, CMDB data accuracy, user satisfaction scores, service request fulfilment SLA compliance.
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT Operations leadership, Help Desk, Change Advisory Board (CAB), Configuration Management team, Finance (asset and licensing), end users, Compliance and Audit.
- **Processes Supported:** Incident management, problem management, change management, CMDB and asset lifecycle, service request management, service catalogue management, continual service improvement.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and maintain the ITSM and CMDB platform product backlog, prioritising enhancements, integrations, and process improvements.
- Define and communicate the platform roadmap aligned to ITIL best practices and organisational service management maturity goals.
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria for ITSM platform features and workflow improvements.
- Collaborate with IT process owners (Incident Manager, Change Manager, Problem Manager) to translate process requirements into platform configuration and automation.
- Work with ServiceNow developers and administrators to deliver backlog items within sprint cycles.
- Govern CMDB data quality: define CMDB scope, CI classes, relationship maps, and data accuracy KPIs.
- Evaluate new ITSM platform capabilities (ServiceNow releases, AI/Now Intelligence features) and define adoption roadmap.
- Manage stakeholder communication including demo sessions, roadmap reviews, and satisfaction surveys.
- Own ITSM tooling licensing strategy and commercial management.
- Define ITSM platform integration requirements with monitoring, alerting, cloud platforms, and DevOps tooling.
- Drive self-service adoption through service catalogue design and virtual agent/chatbot capabilities.

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Working knowledge of ServiceNow (or equivalent ITSM platform) administration and configuration scope.
- Solid understanding of ITIL 4 processes: Incident, Problem, Change, Service Request, Asset, CMDB.
- Experience with Agile/Scrum product ownership and backlog management tools (Jira, Azure DevOps).
- Ability to interpret ITSM reports, dashboards, and operational metrics.
- Familiarity with CMDB concepts: CI classes, discovery methods, data governance.
- Understanding of ITSM integration patterns with monitoring (PagerDuty, Dynatrace, Prometheus), DevOps, and HR systems.

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Strong stakeholder facilitation skills across technical and non-technical audiences.
- Ability to balance competing priorities from multiple IT process owners and business stakeholders.
- Clear communication of platform roadmap and change impact.
- Driven by measurable service improvement rather than feature volume.

## Interactions with Other Roles

> **Interaction Mode** describes the direction/nature of the relationship: **Collaborates** (peer-to-peer), **Consumes From** (relies on the other role's output/service), **Provides To** (delivers a service the other role consumes), **Governed By** (subject to the other role's standards/approval), or **Escalates To** (routes unresolved issues upward).

| Role | Nature of Interaction | Interaction Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Service Management Architect: | Align platform development with ITSM process design and standards | Collaborates |
| ITSM / ConfigMgr Engineers: | Direct the delivery team; provide clear stories and acceptance criteria | Provides To |
| IT Process Owners (Incident, Change, Problem Managers): | Translate process needs into backlog items | Consumes From |
| IT Operations and Help Desk: | Represent their operational requirements in the backlog | Consumes From |
| Finance / Procurement: | Align licensing and tooling costs with budget cycles | Collaborates |

- Running sprint planning and backlog refinement with the ITSM development team.
- Reviewing and triaging new enhancement requests and defect reports.

- Evaluating ServiceNow release notes and planning feature adoption.
- Presenting ITSM platform roadmap and metrics at IT leadership forums.
- Writing acceptance criteria for upcoming sprint user stories.
