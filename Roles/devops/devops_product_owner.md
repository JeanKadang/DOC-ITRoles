## Role Overview

The DevOps Product Owner manages the DevOps platform roadmap and adoption strategy, ensuring CI/CD infrastructure and automation tools meet the needs of development and operations teams. This role prioritizes DevOps capabilities, coordinates implementation schedules, and drives platform adoption across the organization.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — DevOps platform backlog, sprint roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Technical architecture and toolchain design (DevOps Architect-owned); security requirements for pipeline tooling (Security PO-owned); engineering team hiring and resourcing (Chapter Lead-owned)
- **Escalates To:** DevOps & Delivery Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** DevOps Engineers on backlog refinement and delivery-transparency questions

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Maximises the value of the DevOps platform investment by ensuring CI/CD tooling, pipeline capabilities, and automation services are aligned with engineering team needs and measurably improve software delivery performance across the organisation.
- **Value Metrics:** DevOps platform adoption rate, DORA metrics improvement trend (deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate), development team satisfaction score, platform roadmap delivery rate, tooling cost per active user.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO / VP Engineering, development team leads, DevOps Architect, Security Product Owner, FinOps team, HR (developer experience).
- **Processes Supported:** Agile sprint planning and backlog management, DevOps maturity assessment, tooling procurement and license management, developer enablement and training, DORA metrics tracking and reporting.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the DevOps platform service roadmap
- Manage the product backlog for DevOps platform enhancements
- Gather requirements from development and operations teams
- Define acceptance criteria for DevOps tooling and capabilities
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the DevOps team
- Measure and report on DevOps platform adoption and value
- Coordinate DevOps training and enablement initiatives
- Establish service level objectives for DevOps platforms

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| DevOps platform backlog, sprint roadmap, and feature prioritisation | Technical architecture and toolchain design (with DevOps Architect) |
| DevOps tooling license and investment strategy | Security requirements for pipeline tooling (with Security PO) |
| DORA metrics reporting and developer experience improvement backlog | Engineering team hiring and resourcing decisions |
