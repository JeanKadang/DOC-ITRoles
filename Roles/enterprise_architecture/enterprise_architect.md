## Role Overview

The Enterprise Architect develops and maintains the overall technological vision and roadmap for the organization. This role designs comprehensive enterprise-wide architectures that align technology investments with business strategy, ensuring that disparate systems and platforms work together effectively within a cohesive framework.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Develops and maintains the enterprise technology architecture framework ensuring technology investments align with business strategy, deliver cross-domain coherence, and enable organisational agility and innovation
- **Value Metrics:** Business-IT strategic alignment effectiveness, architecture standards adoption rate, technical debt reduction, time-to-market improvements from architectural enablement, cost efficiencies from platform rationalisation
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO/CIO, IT leadership, business leaders and executive sponsors, Product Owners, domain architects, senior technical leaders
- **Processes Supported:** Enterprise architecture governance, technology portfolio management, architecture review board, cross-domain standards development, technology roadmap planning, AI and GenAI governance

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the enterprise architecture framework and standards
- Create technology roadmaps aligned with business strategies and goals
- Design cross-domain architectural patterns and reference architectures
- Govern technology decisions across the organization including AI/GenAI adoption strategies
- Evaluate emerging technologies (AI, GenAI, edge, quantum) for strategic fit and business value
- Lead architecture review boards and governance processes
- Establish architecture principles, policies, and AI governance frameworks
- Mentor domain architects and ensure alignment across architectural domains

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Enterprise architecture framework, cross-domain standards, and technology roadmap governance | Business strategy priorities, investment priorities, and commercial decisions |
| Architecture review board decisions, enterprise architecture principles, and AI/GenAI governance frameworks | Domain-specific technical designs and implementation approaches |
| Enterprise technology portfolio direction, platform selection criteria, and lifecycle standardisation | Individual domain architecture decisions and project-level design choices |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Broad knowledge across multiple technology domains
- Deep understanding of business strategy and processes
- Experience with enterprise architecture frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman, etc.)
- Strong systems thinking and ability to manage complexity
- Experience with technology portfolio management
- Knowledge of industry trends and emerging technologies
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Relevant advanced certifications (TOGAF, AWS/Azure/GCP Enterprise Architect)

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** ArchiMate/LeanIX/Bizzdesign (EA modeling), TOGAF, Azure/AWS/GCP (cloud architecture)
- **Proficient level required:** Azure Integration Services/Kafka (enterprise integration), Azure APIM (API management), business capability mapping frameworks
- **Working Knowledge required:** Enterprise AI governance frameworks, technology portfolio management tools
- **Awareness level expected:** ArchiMate advanced modeling, innovation management systems

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible for all architecture, governance, and advisory activities
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, LeanIX or equivalent enterprise architecture repository, Azure Portal, and roadmapping tools
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for executive architecture forums, major stakeholder workshops, or cross-domain strategy sessions
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-domain reviews, executive briefings, and cross-regional architecture forums
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call for operational incidents; available through escalation for critical architecture governance decisions or cross-domain issues with business impact
