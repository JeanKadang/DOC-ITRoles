## Role Overview

The Enterprise Architecture Senior Engineer operates at the intersection of technical delivery and architectural governance. This role maintains and develops the organization's enterprise architecture repository, tooling, and processes — ensuring that architecture artifacts, patterns, and standards are accurately documented, accessible, and actionable for engineering and architecture teams. The Senior Engineer supports the Enterprise Architect and Solution Architect in running architecture governance processes, building reference architectures, and enabling teams across the organization to apply enterprise architecture standards in their day-to-day work.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the enterprise architecture function operates with high-quality tooling, accurate and current architecture artifacts, and effective governance processes — enabling technology teams to make faster, better-informed decisions that align with organizational standards
- **Value Metrics:** Architecture repository completeness and currency, architecture review turnaround time, reference architecture adoption rate, standards compliance in solution designs, reduction in duplicate or conflicting architecture patterns across domains
- **Key Stakeholders:** Enterprise Architect, Solution Architect, domain architects across all IT areas, CTO/CIO office, delivery teams requiring architecture guidance, and audit/compliance teams
- **Processes Supported:** Architecture repository management, architecture review board preparation and documentation, reference architecture development, standards library maintenance, technology radar publication, and architecture tooling operations

## Key Responsibilities

- Maintain and develop the enterprise architecture repository in tools such as LeanIX, Bizzdesign, ARIS, or equivalent, ensuring accuracy and completeness of capability maps, application landscapes, and technology inventories
- Support the architecture review board process: prepare review packs, capture decisions, maintain the decision log, and track action items through to completion
- Develop and maintain reference architecture documents, architecture patterns, and standards documentation in the architecture knowledge base
- Build and publish the organization's technology radar, tracking emerging and retiring technologies across domains
- Create architecture visualizations, capability maps, and system context diagrams for use in governance forums and stakeholder communications
- Operate and administer enterprise architecture tooling, managing integrations with CMDB, service management, and cloud management platforms
- Support delivery teams in applying enterprise architecture standards — providing guidance on architecture documentation requirements, template usage, and pattern reuse
- Assist the Enterprise Architect in producing architecture reports, maturity assessments, and board-level communications
- Maintain the business capability model, application portfolio inventory, and technology lifecycle records
- Contribute to architecture community of practice activities: knowledge sharing sessions, architecture clinic facilitation, and onboarding guidance for new architects

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Architecture repository data quality, structure, and tooling operations | Enterprise architecture strategy, principles, and governance framework (Enterprise Architect-owned) |
| Reference architecture documentation, pattern library maintenance, and technology radar content | Architecture review decisions and standards approvals (Enterprise and Solution Architect-owned) |
| Architecture review board administration, decision logging, and action tracking | Cross-domain architectural design decisions and technology selection (Architect-owned) |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Hands-on experience with enterprise architecture tooling: LeanIX, Bizzdesign, ARIS, Sparx EA, or equivalent
- Proficiency in architecture modelling languages: ArchiMate, UML, or C4 model
- Ability to produce clear, accurate architecture diagrams and documentation for technical and non-technical audiences
- Understanding of enterprise architecture frameworks: TOGAF concepts, Zachman, or equivalent
- Familiarity with CMDB and IT asset management concepts for integration with the architecture repository
- Experience with diagramming and documentation tools: Miro, draw.io, Confluence, SharePoint
- Strong written communication skills for producing standards documents, reference architectures, and decision records
- Understanding of cloud architectures (AWS, Azure, GCP) at a conceptual level for accurately modeling cloud landscapes
- Ability to manage multiple workstreams, maintain data quality, and meet governance process deadlines

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** ArchiMate/Structurizr C4 model (architecture modeling), LeanIX/Bizzdesign (EA platforms), Miro/draw.io/Lucidchart (diagramming)
- **Proficient level required:** Confluence/SharePoint (documentation), Git/Azure DevOps (version control), Power BI (reporting)
- **Working Knowledge required:** ServiceNow CMDB (ITSM integration), Azure/AWS/GCP (conceptual cloud awareness)
- **Awareness level expected:** Technology radar tooling, AI-assisted architecture tools

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Updating the architecture repository with new applications, capabilities, and technology records
- Preparing architecture review board packs and distributing pre-read materials to reviewers
- Documenting architecture decisions, capturing rationale, and logging outcomes in the decision register
- Creating and refining reference architecture diagrams and pattern documentation
- Publishing updates to the technology radar and standards library
- Supporting domain architects in creating compliant architecture deliverables
- Responding to queries from delivery teams on architecture standards and tooling
- Running data quality checks on the architecture repository and resolving inconsistencies
- Producing architecture landscape reports and capability heat maps for leadership communications
- Facilitating architecture community of practice sessions and maintaining shared knowledge resources

## Key Performance Indicators

- Architecture repository completeness and data quality score
- Architecture review board cycle time (days from submission to decision)
- Reference architecture adoption rate across delivery projects
- Technology radar publication frequency and currency
- Stakeholder satisfaction with architecture governance support and tooling
- Standards documentation coverage (percentage of domains with current reference architectures)
- Action item closure rate from architecture review board decisions
- Architecture community of practice engagement metrics

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; all architecture repository management, documentation, and governance support work is performed through cloud tooling
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, LeanIX or equivalent EA platform, Confluence, SharePoint, Miro, draw.io, and GitHub/ADO
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for architecture governance forums, community of practice events, and cross-domain workshops
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional architecture reviews and governance sessions
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not typically on-call; available for urgent architecture governance support during major delivery decisions or audit cycles

## Career Development Path

**Previous Roles:**

- Solution Designer or Technical Analyst with architecture documentation experience
- Domain Engineer (Cloud, Network, Security) developing an enterprise architecture interest
- Business Analyst with strong systems and technology modelling background
- IT Asset or CMDB Manager transitioning into architecture

**Potential Next Roles:**

- Solution Architect
- Enterprise Architect
- Domain Architect (Cloud, Security, Data, or other domain with aligned interest)
- Architecture Practice Lead or Architecture Office Manager
