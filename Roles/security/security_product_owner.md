## Role Overview

The Security Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's security services portfolio. This role leads a team of security architects and engineers, ensuring that security capabilities meet business requirements, risk management objectives, and compliance needs while aligning with the overall security strategy.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — security programme backlog, investment roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management, ideally with security domain exposure — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Security architecture and technical design (Security Architect-owned); regulatory compliance interpretation (Compliance-owned); enterprise risk appetite setting (Risk Management-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** IT teams on security implementation prioritisation

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the organisation's security investment delivers measurable risk reduction and regulatory compliance by managing the security capability roadmap, aligning security priorities with business risk, and driving adoption of security services across all teams.
- **Value Metrics:** Security risk score trend, compliance achievement rate, security backlog delivery velocity, security capability adoption rate, cost per security incident vs industry benchmark, stakeholder satisfaction with security services.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, CTO, Board Risk Committee, Legal and Compliance, business unit leaders, Security Architect, Risk Management.
- **Processes Supported:** Agile sprint planning and backlog management, security risk register management, regulatory compliance programme, security awareness and training, security tooling procurement, vendor risk management.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for security services
- Prioritize security initiatives based on risk assessment and strategic alignment
- Define acceptance criteria for security deliverables
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the security team
- Engage with business stakeholders to understand security requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for security capabilities
- Track and report on security metrics and value delivery
- Manage security service lifecycle and improvement
- Oversee security governance and compliance initiatives
- Manage privileged access controls and review processes
- Optimize user access experience and efficiency
- Improve access request and approval workflows
- Conduct regular access certification and reviews
- Modernize access management technology
- Develop self-service security capabilities
- Analyze and report on security management metrics

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Security services backlog prioritisation and sprint roadmap | Technical security architecture and control design (with Security Architect) |
| Security investment priorities aligned to risk register and compliance obligations | Risk appetite and acceptance thresholds (with CISO / Risk Management) |
