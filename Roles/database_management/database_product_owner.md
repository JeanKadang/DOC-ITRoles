## Role Overview

The Database Product Owner manages the portfolio of database platforms and data storage solutions across the organization. This role leads a team of database architects and engineers, ensuring that database services meet application requirements, performance standards, security policies, and cost objectives.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — database platform backlog, standardisation roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Database technical architecture and design (Database Architect-owned); database-as-a-service platform selection (Cloud Platform Product Owners-owned, this role aligns to it); database security standards (Security teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Data & AI Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Application Product Owners on data requirements

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the database platform portfolio delivers reliable, secure, and cost-efficient data services enabling fast application development, business intelligence, and data-driven decision-making
- **Value Metrics:** Database platform availability, time to provision database environments, licensing cost optimization, roadmap delivery completion, stakeholder satisfaction with data services
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, application product owners, data governance teams, security, cloud platform POs, finance, IT leadership
- **Processes Supported:** Database backlog and roadmap management, capacity planning, version lifecycle governance, licensing management, agile sprint delivery, stakeholder requirements gathering

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for database platforms and services
- Prioritize database initiatives, upgrades, and migrations based on business impact
- Define acceptance criteria for database platform deliverables
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the database team
- Engage with application teams to understand data storage requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for database technology evolution
- Manage database licensing and capacity planning
- Track and optimize database performance, availability, and cost metrics

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Database platform product backlog prioritization and strategic technology roadmap | Database architecture decisions and platform selection strategy |
| Acceptance criteria and delivery scope for database platform capabilities, upgrades, and migrations | Cloud database adoption strategy and organizational data governance direction |
| Database licensing governance, TCO optimization, and vendor relationship management | Enterprise data architecture and cross-platform integration decisions |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Strong understanding of database technologies (relational and non-relational)
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product ownership
- Background in database administration or engineering
- Knowledge of data governance and management principles
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
- Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities
- Experience with technology evaluation and selection
- Understanding of data security and compliance requirements

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Translates complex database platform capabilities into clear business value for application teams and IT leadership; presents database roadmaps and platform metrics effectively to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Builds strong collaborative relationships with database architects, engineers, application product owners, and data governance teams to align platform delivery with organizational data needs
- Makes evidence-based backlog decisions through analysis of platform usage data, application team needs, licensing cost, performance trends, and technology lifecycle considerations

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

**Expert level required:**

- Database platform product backlog management and technology roadmap (Jira, Confluence)
- database licensing governance and TCO optimisation
- database service level management and stakeholder performance reporting

**Proficient level required:**

- Relational and NoSQL database platform concepts (Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MongoDB)
- Database-as-a-Service adoption governance (Azure SQL, AWS RDS/Aurora, GCP Cloud SQL)
- database lifecycle and version upgrade planning

**Working Knowledge required:**

- Database automation and DevOps tooling concepts
- database security and compliance framework oversight
- cloud database cost management and FinOps reporting

**Awareness level expected:**

- NewSQL and HTAP database trends
- AI-assisted database administration tools
- database-as-code and schema migration practices (Flyway, Liquibase)

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; product ownership is stakeholder communication, backlog management, and strategic coordination
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, database performance dashboards, and DB licensing management portals
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for executive stakeholder sessions, major database migration reviews, or vendor QBRs
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for distributed team agile ceremonies
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call; available through escalation for critical database failures requiring executive-level communication or emergency vendor engagement
