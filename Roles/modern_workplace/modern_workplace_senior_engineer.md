## Role Overview

The Modern Workplace Senior Engineer implements, manages, and optimises the organisation's Microsoft 365 platform workloads at a deep technical level. This role acts as the primary technical specialist and escalation point for Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, and Microsoft Purview compliance capabilities. The Senior Engineer leads complex configuration projects, drives automation of M365 administration, and ensures the platform operates securely, reliably, and in alignment with architecture standards.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — detailed engineering design and delivery within the M365 reference architecture
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in M365/collaboration engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's reference architecture
- **Out of Scope:** M365 tenant architecture and governance standards (Architect-owned); enterprise identity architecture (Identity Engineers-owned); legal hold policy design (Legal/Compliance-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Modern Workplace Architect — architecture-level questions and design direction
- **Escalated To By:** Modern Workplace Engineers on complex M365 configuration and security-event issues

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensure Microsoft 365 platform reliability, security, and user experience, directly supporting workforce productivity and compliance obligations.
- **Value Metrics:** Exchange Online mail flow SLA, Teams call quality metrics, SharePoint performance, compliance policy enforcement coverage, M365 administration automation coverage.
- **Key Stakeholders:** End users, Help Desk, IT Operations, Legal and Compliance, HR.
- **Processes Supported:** Email communications, virtual meetings and telephony, document collaboration, information governance, user provisioning and lifecycle.

## Key Responsibilities

- Administer and maintain Microsoft 365 workloads: Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, and Purview compliance.
- Lead the delivery of M365 configuration projects: tenant migrations, new workload deployments, governance policy implementations.
- Implement and maintain Exchange Online mail flow rules, connectors, transport policies, and email security (DMARC, DKIM, SPF, MDO).
- Configure and manage Teams policies: meeting policies, app policies, calling plans, guest access, and channel governance.
- Implement SharePoint Online site architecture, hub sites, permissions, and information architecture configurations.
- Configure Microsoft Purview: retention labels and policies, sensitivity labels, DLP policies, and eDiscovery cases.
- Develop PowerShell and Microsoft Graph API scripts for M365 administration, reporting, and automation.
- Monitor M365 service health, investigate incidents, and manage Microsoft support cases.
- Implement and tune Defender for Office 365 policies: anti-phishing, safe attachments, safe links.
- Conduct Microsoft 365 Secure Score review cycles and implement improvements.
- Mentor M365 engineers and provide guidance on complex administration scenarios.
- Produce and maintain runbooks, SOPs, and technical documentation.
