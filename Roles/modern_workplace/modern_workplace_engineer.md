## Role Overview

The Modern Workplace Engineer administers and supports the organisation's Microsoft 365 platform on a day-to-day basis. This role manages user mailboxes, Teams configurations, SharePoint permissions, and OneDrive governance while handling escalated support from the help desk. The M365 Engineer is responsible for routine platform operations, user lifecycle administration, and contributing to compliance and security policy maintenance across the Microsoft 365 tenant.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Team — day-to-day M365 operation: provisioning, mailbox/Teams administration, and user support escalations
- **Experience Anchor:** 1-3 years in M365 or collaboration engineering — works under guidance, building toward independent delivery
- **Out of Scope:** M365 tenant architecture and governance standards (Architect-owned); legal hold and eDiscovery policy design (Legal/Compliance directs, this role executes); enterprise security configuration strategy (Senior Engineers/Architect-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Senior M365 Engineers — complex configuration issues and design-level questions
- **Escalated To By:** Help Desk on M365 tickets requiring engineering-tier resolution

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensure reliable, secure, and well-governed access to Microsoft 365 communication and collaboration services for all employees.
- **Value Metrics:** Mailbox provisioning SLA, Teams access provisioning time, compliance policy coverage, help desk escalation resolution time.
- **Key Stakeholders:** End users, Help Desk, HR (onboarding/offboarding), Compliance.
- **Processes Supported:** Employee onboarding/offboarding (mailbox, Teams, SharePoint), email operations, document collaboration management, compliance label enforcement.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provision and manage Exchange Online mailboxes, distribution groups, shared mailboxes, and mail-enabled resources.
- Administer Microsoft Teams: create teams and channels, manage membership, configure policies, and support guest access requests.
- Manage SharePoint Online sites and OneDrive: provisioning, permissions management, and storage governance.
- Action compliance and governance requests: apply sensitivity labels, process eDiscovery holds, and configure retention policies.
- Monitor M365 service health and communicate proactively on service disruptions.
- Resolve escalated help desk tickets related to Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive.
- Maintain M365 user lifecycle: mailbox offboarding, licence recycling, data archiving, and shared mailbox transitions.
- Support Defender for Office 365 operations: release false positive quarantined emails and action threat alerts.
- Maintain knowledge base articles and runbooks for M365 operational procedures.
- Participate in M365 configuration changes following change management processes.

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Processing new user mailbox and Teams access provisioning requests.

- Resolving email delivery failures and NDR investigations.
- Processing external sharing and guest access requests.
- Running eDiscovery content searches for legal holds.
- Monitoring M365 service health and communicating updates.
- Updating runbooks and knowledge base articles.
