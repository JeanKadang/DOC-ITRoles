## Role Overview

The Modern Workplace Architect is responsible for designing, governing, and evolving the organisation's Microsoft 365 and digital workplace platform strategy. This role owns the architecture across Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, Viva, and the Microsoft 365 security and compliance stack - ensuring these platforms are designed to maximise workforce productivity, collaboration, governance, and security at enterprise scale. The Architect leads Microsoft 365 workload migrations, tenant governance design, and the adoption of emerging M365 capabilities including Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — Microsoft 365 tenant architecture and governance standards
- **Experience Anchor:** Minimum 8 years in M365/collaboration engineering, with at least 3 years in an architecture role — operates independently on domain-wide architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Security policy for M365 workloads (with CISO); legal hold and eDiscovery requirements (with Legal); HR processes supporting collaboration tool adoption; licensing strategy and commercial decisions (with Procurement)
- **Escalates To:** End User & Workplace Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities, cross-domain boundary disputes, and decisions beyond M365 platform scope
- **Escalated To By:** Modern Workplace Senior Engineers and Engineers on design-level questions and standards clarification

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Enable a productive, secure, and compliant digital workplace that supports hybrid working, accelerates collaboration, and reduces legacy communication and collaboration platform costs.
- **Value Metrics:** Microsoft 365 adoption rates by workload, email deliverability and availability, Teams meeting reliability, SharePoint/OneDrive compliance posture, licensing cost per seat optimisation, Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption rate.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, HR, Legal and Compliance, Communications, Executive sponsors, End users, Finance (licensing).
- **Processes Supported:** Employee communication and collaboration, document management and retention, information governance, hybrid meeting workflows, external guest collaboration, regulatory compliance (eDiscovery, retention, DLP).

## Key Responsibilities

- Design the organisation's Microsoft 365 tenant architecture: tenant configuration, identity integration with Microsoft Entra ID, and governance policies.
- Architect Exchange Online migration and operations: mail flow design, hybrid configuration, shared mailbox governance, email security (DMARC, DKIM, SPF, Defender for Office 365).
- Design Microsoft Teams platform governance: Teams creation policies, channel architecture, external access, meeting room standards (Teams Rooms), and calls/telephony (Teams Phone).
- Define SharePoint Online and OneDrive information architecture: site design patterns, hub site topology, permissions model, and data lifecycle governance.
- Architect Microsoft 365 information governance: retention policies, sensitivity labels, DLP policies, eDiscovery, and Purview integration.
- Lead design of Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption architecture: readiness assessment, permissions hygiene, sensitivity labelling, and change management.
- Define Microsoft Viva deployment architecture for employee experience: Viva Insights, Viva Engage, Viva Learning.
- Establish Microsoft 365 tenant security baseline aligned to CIS M365 benchmarks and Microsoft Secure Score targets.
- Govern external collaboration policies (Teams guest access, SharePoint external sharing) aligned to security requirements.
- Create and maintain M365 architecture documentation, governance frameworks, and standards.
- Mentor M365 senior engineers and provide technical governance.
