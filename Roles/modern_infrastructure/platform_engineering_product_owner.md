## Role Overview

The Platform Engineering Product Owner manages the roadmap and development of internal developer platforms that enable application teams to build, deploy, and operate their services efficiently. This role leads a team of platform engineers, ensuring that the developer experience meets the needs of application teams while maintaining operational excellence.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the internal developer platform delivers measurable engineering productivity gains enabling application teams to ship features faster, more reliably, and with higher quality aligned to business technology strategy
- **Value Metrics:** Developer satisfaction NPS, deployment frequency improvement, self-service adoption rates, time-to-productivity improvement for new engineers, platform roadmap delivery completion rate
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, Engineering Managers, application product owners, DevOps teams, security, IT leadership
- **Processes Supported:** Platform backlog and roadmap management, developer experience program delivery, agile sprint planning, stakeholder requirements gathering, platform value measurement and reporting

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for internal developer platforms
- Prioritize platform capabilities based on developer needs and business value
- Define acceptance criteria for platform features and enhancements
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the platform team
- Engage with application teams to understand developer experience needs
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for platform evolution
- Establish metrics for measuring platform effectiveness
- Track and report on platform adoption and value delivery

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Platform product backlog prioritization and strategic developer platform roadmap | Platform architecture strategy and technology selection decisions |
| Acceptance criteria and delivery scope for IDP capabilities, self-service features, and golden paths | Application team development practices, DevOps pipeline strategy, and cloud platform integration |
| Developer productivity measurement program design and KPI framework | Organizational engineering efficiency initiatives and FinOps platform cost optimization decisions |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Strong understanding of software development lifecycle and DevOps practices
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product ownership
- Background in platform engineering, DevOps, or software development
- Knowledge of cloud-native technologies and containerization
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication abilities
- Strategic thinking and planning capabilities
- Experience with value stream mapping and optimization
- Understanding of developer experience principles

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Backstage or similar IDP portal at product management and strategic roadmap level, Jira and Confluence for backlog management, sprint planning, and engineering team ceremonies, , NIST CSF metrics and developer productivity measurement frameworks (deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate), Developer satisfaction measurement (NPS surveys, SPACE framework analytics)
- **Proficient level required:** CI/CD pipeline systems (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps) at capability and self-service feature level, Kubernetes and Docker container platforms for informed product decision-making, Self-service frameworks and service catalog design principles, Developer productivity analytics tools (LinearB, Jellyfish, custom DORA dashboards)
- **Working Knowledge required:** Infrastructure as Code platforms (Terraform, Helm) for feature scoping and acceptance criteria, GitOps tools (ArgoCD, Flux) and progressive delivery concepts, Software supply chain security and DevSecOps platform requirements
- **Awareness level expected:** Emerging internal developer platform trends and CNCF ecosystem developments, AI-assisted developer tooling adoption patterns and GitHub Copilot productivity metrics

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; product ownership is stakeholder communication, backlog management, and delivery coordination
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, developer portal dashboards, developer productivity analytics tools
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for strategic planning sessions or key developer experience workshops with engineering leadership
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard hours with flexibility for distributed engineering team ceremonies and cross-regional stakeholder engagement
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call; available for escalation through management chain when critical IDP platform outages affect developer productivity organization-wide
