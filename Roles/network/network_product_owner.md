## Role Overview

The Network Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's network services portfolio. This role leads a team of network architects and engineers, ensuring that network services meet business requirements, performance targets, and security objectives while aligning with organizational strategy.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — network platform backlog, investment roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Network technical architecture (Network Architect-owned); security initiative ownership (Security Product Owner-owned, this role coordinates with it); hybrid cloud network roadmap ownership (Cloud teams-owned, this role coordinates with them)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** business units on network service needs

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the network services portfolio directly enables organisational connectivity, collaboration, and digital growth goals by aligning engineering work to business priorities and delivering predictable, high-quality network services.
- **Value Metrics:** Network service availability SLA achievement, backlog velocity and feature delivery lead time, stakeholder satisfaction score, network CapEx/OpEx spend vs budget, unplanned outage rate trend.
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT Director, application and platform product owners, site and facilities managers, security leadership, finance (CapEx planning).
- **Processes Supported:** Agile sprint planning and backlog management, IT budget and capacity planning cycle, vendor contract renewal, Change Advisory Board input, SLA and service performance reporting.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for network infrastructure services
- Prioritize network initiatives based on business impact and strategic alignment
- Define acceptance criteria for network deliverables and enhancements
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the network team
- Engage with business stakeholders to understand connectivity requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for network technology evolution
- Manage network capacity planning and investment strategy
- Track and report on network performance, availability, and cost metrics

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Network services backlog prioritisation and sprint roadmap | Technical architecture and solution design (with Network Architect) |
| Investment priorities for network technology refresh and expansion | Security policy requirements for network services (with Security PO) |
| Network service level definitions, acceptance criteria, and KPI targets | Vendor contract terms and procurement (with Finance/Procurement) |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Strong understanding of enterprise network technologies and architectures
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product ownership
- Background in network engineering or architecture
- Knowledge of IT service management principles
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication abilities
- Strategic thinking and planning capabilities
- Experience with technology roadmap development
- Understanding of network security and compliance requirements

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Enterprise network service lifecycle management and roadmap planning, Jira/Azure DevOps backlog and sprint management, network performance and capacity planning concepts
- **Proficient level required:** SD-WAN and SASE technology concepts, network security platforms and Zero Trust frameworks, Wi-Fi and wireless network technologies, network analytics and monitoring dashboards
- **Working Knowledge required:** Network automation frameworks (Ansible, Python scripting), cloud connectivity and hybrid network concepts, MPLS and carrier services
- **Awareness level expected:** IoT network management approaches, AI-driven network analytics (AIOps), private 5G and edge networking

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible — role is primarily stakeholder engagement, planning, and reporting with no physical infrastructure dependency.
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, Power BI (service performance reporting).
- **On-Site Requirements:** Occasional on-site for stakeholder workshops, site reviews, or vendor briefings.
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours; availability for sprint ceremonies across distributed teams.
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on call; acts as escalation point for major business-impacting network service failures requiring stakeholder communication.
