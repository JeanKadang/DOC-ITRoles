## Role Overview

The Commvault Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of enterprise backup and recovery solutions using Commvault technology. This role provides technical leadership for complex Commvault deployments, migrations, and operations while working closely with architects to translate data protection strategies into effective implementations.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads complex Commvault implementations and optimisations ensuring enterprise-grade data protection reliability, advanced recovery capabilities, and automation for critical business workloads
- **Value Metrics:** Backup reliability, complex incident resolution time, RPO/RTO testing pass rates, automation coverage, storage efficiency, migration success rates
- **Key Stakeholders:** Commvault Architect, application teams, storage senior engineers, virtualisation senior engineers, Commvault Product Owner
- **Processes Supported:** Complex backup deployments, Commvault performance optimisation, advanced recovery orchestration, backup automation, multi-site replication, DR testing, engineer mentorship

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex Commvault solutions based on architectural guidance
- Develop automation for Commvault operations and reporting
- Lead troubleshooting for critical backup and recovery issues
- Create and maintain Commvault standards and procedures
- Implement advanced recovery strategies for diverse environments
- Optimize Commvault performance and resource utilization
- Design enterprise-scale backup architectures and workflows
- Provide technical mentorship to Commvault Engineers

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).
