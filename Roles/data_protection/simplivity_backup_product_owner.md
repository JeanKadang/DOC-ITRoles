## Role Overview

The SimpliVity Backup Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's backup and recovery services based on HPE SimpliVity's built-in capabilities. This role leads a team focusing on SimpliVity data protection, ensuring services meet business requirements, compliance standards, and operational objectives.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — SimpliVity platform backlog, recovery capability roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** SimpliVity technical architecture (SimpliVity Backup Architect-owned); virtualisation platform roadmap (VMware Product Owner-owned, this role aligns to it); data protection policy definition (compliance and security teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** application owners on recovery point/time objective requirements

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Manages the SimpliVity data protection platform portfolio ensuring backup and recovery capabilities meet business recovery requirements, compliance standards, and SimpliVity operational health targets
- **Value Metrics:** VM backup SLA compliance, RPO/RTO achievement rates, recovery test success rates, backup storage efficiency, roadmap delivery rate
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT leadership, VMware Product Owner, application product owners, compliance teams, business continuity managers, finance
- **Processes Supported:** Backup platform backlog management, recovery test programme governance, compliance reporting, backup capacity planning, agile ceremonies

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for SimpliVity-based data protection services
- Prioritize SimpliVity backup initiatives based on risk assessment and business impact
- Define acceptance criteria for SimpliVity backup solution deliverables
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the SimpliVity backup team
- Engage with business stakeholders to understand data protection requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for SimpliVity backup capabilities
- Manage capacity planning for SimpliVity backup resources
- Track and report on backup success rates, recovery testing, and SLA compliance

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).
