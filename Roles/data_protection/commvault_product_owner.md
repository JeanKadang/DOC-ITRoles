## Role Overview

The Commvault Product Owner is responsible for maximizing the value of the organization's Commvault data protection platform. This role defines and prioritizes the backlog of features, enhancements, and improvements for the Commvault environment, ensuring it meets business needs, compliance requirements, and operational efficiency goals.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — Commvault platform backlog, recovery capability roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Commvault technical architecture (Commvault Architect-owned); recovery planning strategy (Business Continuity Managers-owned, this role aligns backlog to it); data protection compliance policy (Security Teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Commvault Engineers on implementation and operations status

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Maximises the value of the Commvault data protection platform through a well-prioritised roadmap ensuring reliable backup services, RPO/RTO compliance, and cost-effective data protection aligned with business recovery objectives
- **Value Metrics:** Backup SLA compliance, data recovery success rates, platform availability, license cost efficiency, roadmap delivery rate, compliance audit pass rates
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT leadership, application product owners, compliance and audit teams, security, business continuity managers, finance
- **Processes Supported:** Backup platform backlog management, recovery testing programme, compliance reporting, capacity planning, vendor and license management, agile sprint ceremonies

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and prioritize the backlog for Commvault services and improvements
- Represent business needs and stakeholder requirements for data protection
- Set clear acceptance criteria for Commvault-related work
- Balance technical debt with new feature implementation
- Monitor service levels and identify opportunities for improvement
- Align Commvault capabilities with overall IT strategy
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communications
- Work with technical teams to implement data protection solutions
- Ensure proper documentation of service offerings and capabilities
- Coordinate version upgrades and new feature implementations

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities
