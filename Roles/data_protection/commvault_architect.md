## Role Overview

The Commvault Architect designs enterprise data protection strategies and solutions using Commvault technology. This role creates comprehensive backup, recovery, and data management architectures that ensure data availability, compliance, and business continuity while optimizing for performance and cost efficiency.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — Commvault enterprise backup platform architecture and technology standards across the chapter
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in backup/data protection architecture with demonstrated Commvault platform ownership — operates independently on domain-wide Commvault architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Underlying backup storage architecture (Storage Architect-owned, this role aligns backup strategy to it); SimpliVity hyperconverged backup architecture (SimpliVity Backup Architect-owned — a parallel, non-Commvault backup ladder); data retention regulatory interpretation (compliance teams-owned, this role implements requirements)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities and cross-domain investment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** Commvault Senior Engineers on implementation approaches

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs enterprise Commvault data protection architectures ensuring RPO/RTO objectives are met, compliance requirements are fulfilled, and backup storage is cost-efficiently managed across all workload types
- **Value Metrics:** Backup SLA achievement rates, RPO/RTO compliance, storage deduplication and compression efficiency, data protection architecture adoption rates, architecture quality assessments
- **Key Stakeholders:** IT leadership, application architects, storage architects, security and compliance teams, business continuity managers
- **Processes Supported:** Data protection architecture design, retention policy governance, DR architecture, backup security design, backup capacity planning, technology evaluation

## Key Responsibilities

- Design comprehensive enterprise backup and recovery architectures
- Develop data protection policies, retention strategies, and recovery objectives
- Create scalable Commvault deployment architectures
- Optimize data protection workflows for complex enterprise environments
- Design disaster recovery and business continuity solutions
- Plan storage strategies for backup repositories and archives
- Evaluate new Commvault features and capabilities for adoption
- Provide technical leadership for Commvault implementation projects
