## Role Overview

The High-Performance Computing (HPC) Product Owner leads the development, delivery, and continuous improvement of HPC solutions that support the organization's computationally intensive workloads. This role drives the strategic vision for HPC, ensuring environments are innovative, scalable, secure, and aligned with research and business needs. The Product Owner champions user engagement, value delivery, and adoption of emerging HPC technologies, including cloud and hybrid solutions. The Product Owner also champions sustainability, diversity, and user advocacy, ensuring that HPC solutions are accessible and valuable to all stakeholders.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — HPC platform backlog, investment strategy, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management, ideally with research computing exposure — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** HPC technical architecture (HPC Architect-owned); server hardware procurement roadmap (Server Hardware Product Owner-owned, this role aligns to it); cloud integration compliance detail (Security Teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Data Science and Research departments on evolving computational needs

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Manages the HPC platform backlog and strategic roadmap ensuring cluster capacity, scheduling capabilities, and user services evolve to meet research, data science, and engineering workload requirements, enabling the organisation to deliver computational outcomes on schedule
- **Value Metrics:** HPC cluster availability, researcher satisfaction scores, job scheduling efficiency and throughput, roadmap delivery velocity, cost per compute hour, self-service adoption rate, and user onboarding time
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO, Research Directors, Data Science leads, HPC Architect, Server Hardware PO, Cloud POs, FinOps team, research computing users
- **Processes Supported:** HPC backlog management and sprint planning, capacity planning and procurement, agile ceremonies, user community engagement, SLA governance, HPC technology roadmap, cost reporting and FinOps alignment

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop, prioritize, and maintain the product backlog for HPC infrastructure and services
- Define and communicate the strategic roadmap for HPC evolution, including cloud and hybrid capabilities
- Lead sprint planning, agile ceremonies, and cross-functional collaboration for the HPC team
- Engage with scientific, engineering, and analytics stakeholders to understand and anticipate computational needs
- Define acceptance criteria and success metrics for HPC deliverables and enhancements
- Manage capacity planning, resource allocation, and cost optimization for HPC environments
- Track and report on performance, value delivery, and user satisfaction of HPC investments
- Foster a culture of innovation, automation, and continuous improvement in HPC services
- Ensure compliance with security, data protection, and regulatory requirements
- Advocate for sustainability and diversity in HPC strategy and operations
- Lead change management and communication for HPC initiatives
- Gather and act on continuous feedback from users and stakeholders

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

| Owns | Advises On |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Strong understanding of high-performance computing concepts, technologies, and trends
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product management in technical environments
- Background in computational science, engineering, or HPC administration
- Knowledge of research and development processes and user engagement strategies
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and strategic planning skills
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities
- Experience with budget management, cost optimization, and technology procurement
- Familiarity with cloud HPC, automation, and security best practices
- Experience with change management and user advocacy
- Commitment to sustainability and diversity in technology environments

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates HPC strategy and roadmap progress clearly to research directors, IT leadership, and technical teams, bridging scientific requirements with engineering delivery
- Collaborates across research, cloud, hardware, and security teams to build a coordinated HPC service that meets complex multi-stakeholder needs
- Applies data-driven analytical thinking to cluster utilisation patterns and researcher feedback to prioritise the highest-value backlog items

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** HPC cluster platform capabilities (Slurm, PBS Pro, job schedulers) at product planning and SLA governance level, Jira and Confluence for backlog management, research stakeholder engagement, and sprint planning, Cluster utilisation dashboards (Grafana, Prometheus) and reporting tools for data-driven capacity planning, ServiceNow and ITSM platforms for HPC service request and incident governance
- **Proficient level required:** Cloud HPC platforms (AWS ParallelCluster, Azure CycleCloud) product capabilities and hybrid burst use case planning, GPU and accelerator allocation governance and cost-per-compute-hour reporting frameworks, Power BI and analytics tools for cluster utilisation metrics and researcher satisfaction measurement
- **Working Knowledge required:** Parallel file systems (Lustre, GPFS) and HPC storage service capabilities for service catalog design, MPI-based workload requirements and parallel application resource needs for capacity planning, HPC container technologies (Singularity, Apptainer) for reproducible research enablement
- **Awareness level expected:** Emerging quantum computing platforms and future HPC technology directions, NVIDIA H100/Blackwell and AMD Instinct accelerator roadmaps for capacity planning

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible for all backlog management, planning, and stakeholder engagement activities; occasional on-site visits for research community meetings and data centre tours to understand operational constraints
- **Collaboration Tools:** Teams, Jira, Confluence, cluster usage dashboards, Power BI, ServiceNow
- **On-Site Requirements:** On-site visits recommended for major capacity expansion reviews and annual researcher community engagement events
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard core hours with flexibility to engage research stakeholders in different time zones
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Not on-call; receives operational escalation briefings from HPC Senior Engineers for major platform incidents
