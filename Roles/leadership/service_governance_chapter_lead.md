## Role Overview

The Service & Governance Chapter Lead is the most senior technical manager and people leader for the organisation's IT service management, enterprise architecture, and governance capability, spanning four domains: ITSM & Configuration, Service Management, Infrastructure Onboarding, and Enterprise Architecture. This role combines direct line management of architects and senior engineers across these domains with authoritative ownership of the IT service management framework, enterprise architecture governance, CMDB integrity, and the processes that make IT run reliably. The Chapter Lead bridges technology and business — owning ITIL process maturity, service catalogue governance, EA governance board operations, and the cross-domain onboarding standards that ensure new infrastructure and services are adopted into the organisation consistently. Reporting to a Technical Area Lead or Product Area Lead, this role is the definitive "Head of Service & Governance" voice below the TAL layer.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the organisation's IT operates with process discipline, service accountability, and governance maturity — through a well-managed ITSM framework, a trustworthy CMDB estate, a functioning enterprise architecture governance practice, and consistent onboarding standards that reduce service risk and improve IT reliability
- **Value Metrics:** ITSM process maturity level (ITIL 4 capability), CMDB accuracy and completeness rate, enterprise architecture governance compliance rate, onboarding playbook adherence rate, service catalogue coverage, IT compliance reporting accuracy, chapter practitioner retention
- **Key Stakeholders:** Technical Area Lead / Product Area Lead (line manager), domain architects and senior engineers within the chapter, Enterprise Architect, CTO/CIO, all other Chapter Leads (primary consumers of ITSM processes and onboarding standards), business stakeholders requiring IT service accountability, Risk and Audit functions
- **Processes Supported:** ITSM framework (incident, change, problem, request, asset management), CMDB management and integrity, enterprise architecture governance board, service catalogue management, infrastructure onboarding processes, IT compliance and audit reporting, ITIL 4 process maturity improvement

## Key Responsibilities

- Line manage and develop architects and senior engineers across all four chapter domains, including performance management, career development, and chapter hiring
- Own and maintain the chapter career development framework for ITSM, service management, onboarding, and enterprise architecture practitioners, running regular 1:1s, development reviews, and talent calibration
- Lead chapter-level hiring: define role standards, conduct or chair technical interviews, and ensure a consistent engineering bar across ITSM, service management, infrastructure onboarding, and enterprise architecture domains
- Own the ITSM framework — governing all ITIL 4 processes (incident, problem, change, request, asset, and configuration management) and driving process maturity improvement across the organisation
- Own CMDB integrity — defining configuration management standards, CMDB data quality governance, and the processes that ensure the configuration estate accurately reflects the live technology environment
- Operate and continuously improve the enterprise architecture governance board — setting submission standards, review cadences, and architecture decision record (ADR) processes to ensure EA governance is effective and efficient
- Own the service catalogue — ensuring services are accurately defined, owned, and maintained, and that the catalogue is the authoritative reference for IT service accountability and reporting
- Own cross-domain infrastructure onboarding standards — defining the playbooks, checklists, and architecture gates that ensure new infrastructure, services, and platforms are consistently adopted into the operational estate
- Lead IT compliance reporting — ensuring the organisation can accurately report on IT service metrics, configuration estate, and governance adherence to Risk, Audit, and regulatory stakeholders
- Represent the chapter in cross-chapter governance forums, enterprise architecture reviews, and business-facing IT service accountability discussions

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| ITSM framework standards — ITIL 4 process design, process governance, and process maturity improvement programme | IT tool procurement, ITSM platform selection, and vendor contracts (PAL and commercial-owned) |
| CMDB governance — configuration management standards, CMDB data quality rules, and asset management policy | IT asset procurement and lifecycle investment decisions (PAL and Finance-owned) |
| Enterprise architecture governance board — submission standards, review cadence, ADR process, and architecture compliance gate design | Enterprise architecture strategy and long-range IT architecture direction (Enterprise Architect and CTO/CIO-owned) |
| Practitioner career decisions — promotions, development plans, performance management, and hiring bar for the chapter | Compensation bands, HR policy, and organisational structure (PAL and HR-owned) |
| Infrastructure onboarding playbook standards and architecture gate criteria for new services | Technology investment decisions, programme governance, and business case approval (PAL and Finance-owned) |

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep technical expertise in IT service management frameworks (ITIL 4), configuration management, and CMDB governance at enterprise scale
- Proven ability to design and operate enterprise architecture governance processes, including ADR frameworks, architecture review boards, and EA tooling
- Strong understanding of infrastructure onboarding processes, service transition practices, and operational readiness frameworks
- Experience with ITSM tooling at enterprise scale: ServiceNow, BMC Helix, Jira Service Management, or equivalent
- Ability to design and govern service catalogues, SLA frameworks, and IT compliance reporting for enterprise organisations

**Leadership and People Management:**

- Demonstrated line management experience with architect-level or senior engineer practitioners across ITSM, service management, and governance disciplines
- Ability to build and sustain high-performing chapters spanning ITSM process governance, configuration management, infrastructure onboarding, and enterprise architecture
- Experience designing and running technical hiring processes for service management, governance, and enterprise architecture roles
- Skilled at engaging business stakeholders, Risk and Audit functions, and CTO/CIO-level leadership on service accountability, governance maturity, and IT compliance
- Ability to represent service and governance interests at senior leadership level, bridging technology and business perspectives

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** ServiceNow at platform governance and architecture depth
- **Proficient level required:** ITIL 4 / SIAM framework expertise
- **Working Knowledge required:** Enterprise architecture tools (LeanIX, Ardoq) at governance depth
- **Awareness level expected:** Power BI/Tableau for service performance reporting

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in IT service management, enterprise architecture governance, configuration management, or senior IT operations roles
- 3+ years in a Principal Architect, Lead ITSM Architect, or senior technical leadership role with cross-domain governance scope
- Demonstrated experience managing or mentoring architects and senior engineers in a formal or informal leadership capacity
- Track record of owning ITSM process maturity improvement, CMDB governance, or enterprise architecture governance at enterprise scale
- Experience engaging with Risk, Audit, and Compliance stakeholders on IT compliance reporting and service accountability

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Running chapter 1:1s with domain architects and senior engineers, covering ITSM process challenges, CMDB data quality issues, onboarding blockers, and career development
- Chairing or facilitating EA governance board sessions — reviewing architecture submissions, issuing ADRs, and ensuring governance decisions are documented and communicated
- Engaging with the TAL/PAL on chapter roadmap priorities, ITSM process improvement investment, and governance maturity milestones
- Reviewing CMDB data quality reports and working with chapter practitioners to resolve data gaps, orphaned records, and inaccurate configuration items
- Collaborating with all other Chapter Leads on change management processes, infrastructure onboarding gate compliance, and service catalogue updates
- Conducting or calibrating technical interviews for ITSM architect, service management architect, and enterprise architecture roles
- Working with Risk and Audit on IT compliance reporting, audit evidence provision, and governance assurance
- Reviewing and improving the infrastructure onboarding playbook — incorporating lessons learned from recent onboarding activity across all technology chapters
- Participating in cross-chapter governance and enterprise architecture forums to represent service and governance chapter interests
- Mentoring domain architects on ITSM process design, EA governance, and technical leadership development

## Key Performance Indicators

- Chapter practitioner retention rate and voluntary attrition trend
- Hiring velocity and quality for chapter architect and senior engineer roles (90-day retention of new hires)
- Chapter satisfaction score (internal survey results for the Service & Governance chapter)
- ITSM process maturity level across all ITIL 4 processes (measured against ITIL 4 maturity model)
- CMDB accuracy and completeness rate for critical configuration item classes
- EA governance board throughput — submission-to-decision cycle time and architecture compliance rate
- Service catalogue coverage — percentage of IT services with accurate, maintained catalogue entries
- Infrastructure onboarding playbook adherence rate across chapters
- IT compliance reporting accuracy and on-time delivery rate
- Change management success rate and change-related incident trend

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; governance, service management, and people leadership responsibilities benefit from regular in-person engagement with the chapter, TAL/PAL, and cross-chapter stakeholders
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, ServiceNow, LeanIX, Confluence, Miro; ITSM dashboards and EA tooling for distributed visibility of governance and service performance
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular on-site presence required for chapter meetings, EA governance board sessions, cross-chapter governance forums, 1:1s, and CTO/CIO-level reporting; on-site presence for major ITSM platform changes or audit activities
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional governance reviews and compliance reporting cycles
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Available for escalation on critical ITSM process failures or CMDB data integrity events affecting incident or change management; participates in post-incident reviews for major incidents where ITSM process gaps are identified

## Career Development Path

**Previous Roles:**

- Principal Enterprise Architect or Lead ITSM Architect with cross-domain governance breadth
- Senior Service Management Architect or Configuration Manager with governance and process leadership experience
- Head of IT Service Management or ITSM Process Owner with architecture and people leadership track record

- VP of IT Operations or Director of IT Governance
