## Role Overview

The End User & Workplace Chapter Lead is the most senior technical manager and people leader for the organisation's end-user technology capability, spanning three domains: Client Platform, Endpoint Management, and Modern Workplace. This role combines direct line management of architects and senior engineers in these domains with authoritative ownership of the client OS engineering strategy, device management platform, Microsoft 365 governance, collaboration tooling architecture, and the digital workplace roadmap. The Chapter Lead is accountable for delivering a consistent, secure, and productive user experience across all endpoints and workplace platforms — from client OS image engineering for Lenovo ThinkPad/ThinkCentre, MacBook, and Linux laptop fleets through to robust endpoint management and M365 governance — balancing technology innovation in the employee experience with strong endpoint security posture, managed in close collaboration with the Security & Identity Chapter Lead. Reporting to a Technical Area Lead or Product Area Lead, this role is the definitive "Head of End User Technology" voice below the TAL layer.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures every employee has access to a secure, productive, and modern digital workplace — through coherent device management standards, well-governed Microsoft 365 platform usage, and a continuously improving collaboration and end-user experience that supports hybrid and flexible working
- **Value Metrics:** Device management coverage and compliance rate, M365 feature adoption and governance compliance, digital employee experience (DEX) score, endpoint security posture alignment with Security chapter standards, modern workplace roadmap delivery milestones, chapter practitioner retention
- **Key Stakeholders:** Technical Area Lead / Product Area Lead (line manager), domain architects and senior engineers within the chapter, Security & Identity Chapter Lead, HR and People function, IT Service Management leads, CTO/CIO, business unit leaders
- **Processes Supported:** Device management strategy and governance, Microsoft 365 platform governance, collaboration tooling and Teams architecture, endpoint security posture management, digital employee experience programme, modern workplace roadmap, hardware lifecycle and device refresh

## Key Responsibilities

- Line manage and develop architects and senior engineers across the Client Platform, Endpoint Management, and Modern Workplace domains, including performance management, career development, and chapter hiring
- Own and maintain the chapter career development framework for end-user and workplace practitioners, running regular 1:1s, development reviews, and talent calibration
- Lead chapter-level hiring: define role standards, conduct or chair technical interviews, and ensure a consistent engineering bar across client platform, endpoint management, and modern workplace domains
- Own the client platform strategy — governing OS engineering for Windows 10/11, macOS (Apple Silicon), and Linux desktop (Ubuntu LTS) across the Lenovo-primary fleet (ThinkPad, ThinkCentre) and MacBook estate; covering image build pipelines, hardware lifecycle, Lenovo driver and firmware governance, and emerging Linux-on-Lenovo adoption
- Own the device management strategy — governing Microsoft Intune, endpoint configuration standards, device compliance policies, and device lifecycle management across all form factors (laptops, desktops, mobile, shared devices)
- Lead the Microsoft 365 platform governance framework — defining M365 tenant architecture standards, Teams governance policies, SharePoint information architecture, and platform adoption roadmap
- Own the digital employee experience (DEX) strategy — using workplace analytics and experience data to identify and drive improvements in end-user technology satisfaction and productivity
- Ensure endpoint security posture is coherent and aligned with the Security & Identity Chapter Lead's standards — governing device compliance, EDR coverage, conditional access integration, and zero trust endpoint requirements
- Lead the collaboration tooling architecture — governing Microsoft Teams, unified communications, and emerging workplace productivity platforms
- Own the modern workplace roadmap — translating business requirements for hybrid working, productivity, and employee experience into a technically sound and delivery-sequenced chapter plan
- Represent the chapter in enterprise architecture forums, security governance discussions (for endpoint posture), and HR/People technology forums

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep technical expertise in Microsoft 365 platform architecture and governance, with strong breadth across endpoint management, unified communications, and digital workplace tooling
- Proven ability to design and govern Microsoft Intune-based device management at enterprise scale, including device compliance, configuration profiles, and app management
- Strong understanding of Microsoft Teams architecture — governance models, calling and telephony, meeting room systems, and Teams platform extensibility
- Experience with digital employee experience (DEX) platforms and workplace analytics tooling
- Familiarity with zero trust endpoint principles — conditional access, device health attestation, EDR integration, and endpoint compliance policy design

**Leadership and People Management:**

- Demonstrated line management experience with architect-level or senior engineer practitioners in end-user and workplace domains
- Ability to build and sustain a high-performing chapter spanning endpoint management and modern workplace specialisms
- Experience designing and running technical hiring processes for end-user technology roles
- Skilled at engaging HR/People stakeholders, business unit leaders, and senior executives on end-user technology strategy and employee experience
- Ability to represent workplace technology interests at senior leadership level and translate digital workplace strategy for non-technical business audiences

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Microsoft 365 at administrative and governance depth
- **Proficient level required:** Microsoft Intune/Endpoint Manager at strategy depth
- **Working Knowledge required:** Collaboration analytics (Viva Insights, Teams analytics) for workplace metrics
- **Awareness level expected:** ITSM platforms (ServiceNow) for end-user service governance

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in endpoint management, Microsoft 365 architecture, modern workplace engineering, or senior end-user technology roles

- Demonstrated experience managing or mentoring architects and senior engineers in a formal or informal leadership capacity
- Track record of owning Microsoft 365 governance, device management strategy, or digital workplace transformation programmes at enterprise scale
- Experience collaborating with security teams to govern endpoint security posture, conditional access, and zero trust device compliance

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; people management responsibilities and end-user technology oversight benefit from regular in-person engagement with the chapter and business stakeholders
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 admin centre, Intune, Endpoint Manager, Confluence; full leverage of the M365 toolset for async and collaborative working — this chapter leads by example on modern workplace adoption
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular on-site presence required for chapter meetings, 1:1s, and cross-chapter leadership forums; on-site presence for major device refresh deployments, meeting room technology rollouts, and employee experience initiatives
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-site or cross-regional end-user technology rollouts and governance reviews
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Available for escalation on critical endpoint management or M365 platform incidents affecting business productivity; participates in post-incident reviews for end-user platform outages
