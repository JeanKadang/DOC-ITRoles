## Role Overview

The DevOps & Delivery Chapter Lead is the most senior technical manager and people leader for the organisation's software delivery capability, spanning three domains: DevOps, App Platforms, and Integration & Middleware. This role combines direct line management of architects and senior engineers across these domains with authoritative technical direction over the delivery toolchain, platform engineering maturity, and integration platform governance. The Chapter Lead owns the delivery toolchain strategy — CI/CD, Internal Developer Platforms (IDP), and pipeline architecture — while also governing API standards. Cloud cost discipline for delivery workloads is a close collaboration with the Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead who owns FinOps. Reporting to a Technical Area Lead or Product Area Lead, this role is the definitive technical voice for how software is built and integrated across the organisation.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Cross-domain, chapter-wide — line management and technical authority across delivery and integration domains
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in senior technical roles with 2+ years of demonstrated line management or formal mentorship of architect-level practitioners — operates independently across the full chapter scope
- **Out of Scope:** Enterprise-wide architecture policy and cross-chapter standards (Enterprise Architect-owned); platform infrastructure decisions and cloud service selection (Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead-owned); compensation bands, HR policy, and organisational structure (PAL and HR-owned); cloud spend and FinOps governance (Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Technical Area Lead (TAL) or Product Area Lead (PAL) — strategic priorities, budget allocation, and decisions beyond chapter-level authority
- **Escalated To By:** Domain Architects and Senior Engineers within the chapter on cross-domain technical conflicts, standards disputes, or decisions requiring chapter-wide authority

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Drives software delivery excellence, platform engineering maturity, and cloud cost discipline across the organisation — ensuring development teams can deliver quickly and reliably through standardised toolchains and platforms, while maintaining cost-optimised cloud consumption
- **Value Metrics:** DORA metrics (deployment frequency, lead time for change, change failure rate, mean time to recovery) at chapter level, IDP adoption rate, API governance compliance, pipeline standardisation coverage, chapter practitioner retention
- **Key Stakeholders:** Technical Area Lead / Product Area Lead (line manager), domain architects and senior engineers within the chapter, Cloud & Platform Infrastructure Chapter Lead, Enterprise Architect, Finance stakeholders
- **Processes Supported:** CI/CD pipeline governance, internal developer platform strategy, integration and middleware platform oversight, API lifecycle management, delivery metrics and engineering productivity tracking

## Key Responsibilities

- Line manage and develop architects and senior engineers across all three chapter domains, including performance management, career development, and chapter hiring
- Own and maintain the chapter career development framework for DevOps, App Platforms, and Integration practitioners, running regular 1:1s, development reviews, and talent calibration
- Lead chapter-level hiring: define role standards, conduct or chair technical interviews, and ensure a consistent engineering bar across all four domains
- Own the delivery toolchain strategy — defining standards for CI/CD platforms, pipeline architecture, and developer tooling across the organisation
- Drive the Internal Developer Platform (IDP) strategy, governing platform engineering maturity and the self-service capabilities available to development teams
- Lead API governance: own integration platform standards, middleware architecture, and API lifecycle policies that ensure interoperability and reduce integration complexity
- Collaborate with the Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead on cloud-native platform decisions and delivery workload cost governance (FinOps is owned within that chapter)
- Track and report DORA metrics at chapter level, using delivery performance data to identify and drive improvements in engineering flow and quality
- Own the chapter roadmap — translating TAL/PAL strategic direction into delivery toolchain and platform engineering priorities
- Represent the chapter in enterprise architecture forums, cloud strategy forums, and financial governance discussions

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep technical expertise in DevOps practices, CI/CD platforms, and platform engineering, with strong breadth across integration, middleware, and cloud financial management
- Proven ability to design and govern delivery toolchains at enterprise scale, including IDP architecture and developer experience optimisation
- Strong understanding of integration patterns: REST, event-driven architecture, message brokers, API management platforms, and enterprise service bus patterns
- Experience leading FinOps practice implementation — cloud tagging, cost allocation, showback/chargeback models, and spend optimisation
- Familiarity with DORA metrics, engineering productivity measurement, and continuous improvement frameworks for software delivery

**Leadership and People Management:**

- Demonstrated line management experience with architect-level or senior engineer practitioners across delivery and platform domains
- Ability to build and sustain high-performing chapters spanning diverse delivery disciplines (DevOps, platform engineering, integration, FinOps)
- Experience designing and running technical hiring processes, including calibration of engineering bars across delivery-focused domains
- Skilled at facilitating chapter governance, architecture reviews, and cross-domain delivery improvement initiatives
- Ability to represent delivery and platform interests at senior leadership level, including financial governance with Finance and procurement stakeholders

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

**Expert level required:**

- GitHub Actions/Azure DevOps at governance depth

**Proficient level required:**

- Internal Developer Platform tooling (Backstage, Port) for strategy

**Working Knowledge required:**

- DORA metrics tooling for chapter performance visibility

**Awareness level expected:**

- Integration platforms (Azure API Management, MuleSoft) at architecture depth

- Demonstrated experience managing or mentoring architects and senior engineers in a formal or informal leadership capacity
- Track record of owning delivery toolchain strategy and platform engineering governance at enterprise scale
- Experience establishing or maturing a FinOps practice, including cloud cost governance and optimisation programmes

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; people management responsibilities and chapter leadership require regular in-person engagement with practitioners and senior stakeholders
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, GitHub/Azure DevOps, Confluence, Miro, LeanIX; async-first communication for distributed chapter practitioners; FinOps dashboards and DORA tooling for distributed visibility
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular on-site presence required for chapter meetings, architecture governance sessions, 1:1s, and cross-chapter leadership forums; on-site presence for major delivery toolchain rollouts and platform migrations
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional pipeline governance and cloud spend reviews
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Available for escalation on critical pipeline or integration platform incidents; participates in post-incident reviews for delivery platform outages and drives systemic improvements
