## Role Overview

The SVP of Technology (Senior Vice President of Technology) is the most senior technology executive in the organisation, accountable to the CEO and Board of Directors for the full IT and technology strategy, investment, capability, and delivery. The SVP owns the organisation's technology vision and multi-year roadmap, holds budget authority across all technology investment, and represents technology interests at the executive leadership level. In some organisations this role is held by a **CTO (Chief Technology Officer)** or **CIO (Chief Information Officer)** rather than an SVP — these titles are considered equivalent at this level, and the responsibilities described here apply equally to all three. In some organisations, the SVP of Technology and the CISO role may be held by the same person; this is noted where relevant.

The SVP of Technology sits above the Product Area Lead (PAL) and Technical Area Lead (TAL) in the technology leadership hierarchy, providing the strategic direction and organisational context within which PALs and TALs operate.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures technology strategy, investment, and delivery are fully aligned with organisational objectives, enabling sustainable competitive advantage, operational resilience, and digital innovation across the enterprise
- **Value Metrics:** Technology return on investment, digital transformation programme delivery, enterprise technology risk posture, IT spend as a percentage of revenue, technology talent retention, board confidence in technology governance
- **Key Stakeholders:** CEO, Board of Directors (and Board Risk or Audit Committee), CFO, COO, Chief People Officer, business unit executives, Product Area Leads, Technical Area Lead, CISO, Enterprise Architect, and key strategic technology vendors and partners
- **Processes Supported:** Enterprise technology strategy and planning, board-level risk and investment reporting, IT governance and policy, annual budget and multi-year investment planning, digital transformation programme governance, organisational design for the technology function, vendor and partner executive relationship management

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and communicate the enterprise technology strategy and multi-year roadmap, ensuring alignment with business strategy and evolving market conditions
- Hold full budget authority for all technology investment, including capital expenditure, operating expenditure, headcount, and third-party spend
- Report to the CEO and Board of Directors on technology risk, investment performance, digital transformation progress, and strategic capability
- Chair the IT leadership forum and technology governance bodies, ensuring cross-area alignment and portfolio coherence
- Represent technology interests and perspectives in the executive leadership team, influencing organisational strategy and investment decisions
- Own the organisational design for the technology function, including the PAL/TAL leadership model, domain structure, and capability planning
- Drive the digital transformation and innovation agenda for the organisation, building the partnerships and investment cases required to deliver change at scale
- Manage executive relationships with strategic vendors and technology partners, including negotiation of enterprise agreements and strategic partnerships
- Set the tone for technology risk appetite, ensuring the organisation's risk posture is appropriate, transparent, and governed effectively
- Develop and retain the senior technology leadership team, building succession depth and a high-performance culture across the function

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Enterprise technology strategy, vision, and multi-year roadmap | Overall corporate strategy and business investment priorities (CEO and Board-owned) |
| Total technology budget authority: capital, operating, headcount, and vendor spend | Corporate financial policy, treasury, and enterprise-wide budget frameworks (CFO-owned) |
| Organisational design for the technology function, including leadership structure and role design | Enterprise-wide people policies, HR frameworks, and executive compensation (CPO and Board-owned) |
| Technology risk appetite definition and board-level risk reporting | Enterprise risk framework and non-technology risk categories (CRO and Board-owned) |
| Strategic vendor and partner relationships, enterprise agreement negotiation, and partnership decisions | Corporate procurement policy and enterprise-wide supplier governance (CPO/COO-owned) |
| Digital transformation programme mandate, investment, and executive sponsorship | Programme delivery execution (PAL and delivery leadership-owned) |
| Senior technology leadership hiring, structure, and succession planning | Executive appointments outside the technology function (CEO and Board-owned) |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Leadership and Executive Management:**

- Proven track record as a senior technology executive (CTO, CIO, SVP, or VP of Technology) in a complex, multi-domain technology organisation
- Deep experience in technology strategy development, multi-year investment planning, and board-level governance
- Strong financial acumen — able to own and manage large, multi-disciplinary technology budgets with rigour and transparency
- Exceptional stakeholder management at executive and board level, including the ability to translate complex technology topics for non-technical audiences
- Experience leading organisational transformation, including technology function redesign, culture change, and large-scale digital programmes
- Track record of building and retaining high-performing, senior technology leadership teams

**Technical Literacy:**

- Sufficient depth across cloud, security, data, software engineering, and enterprise architecture to engage credibly with technical leaders and make informed strategic decisions
- Understanding of enterprise architecture, technology risk management, cybersecurity principles, and regulatory compliance in a technology context
- Familiarity with emerging technology trends, including AI/ML, cloud-native platforms, and digital product development, sufficient to set credible innovation direction

**Soft Skills:**

- Strategic thinking and the ability to set clear direction in conditions of ambiguity and rapid change
- Executive communication and influencing skills — confident representing technology at board level and in media or external forums
- Collaborative leadership style that empowers PALs, TALs, and domain leaders while maintaining clear accountability
- Resilience and decisiveness, particularly in high-stakes technology incidents, major investment decisions, and transformation inflection points

## Qualifications

- Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business, or a related field; postgraduate qualification (MBA, MSc) advantageous
- 15+ years of progressive technology leadership experience, with at least 5 years in a senior executive role (CTO, CIO, VP, or SVP level)
- Demonstrable experience of board engagement, technology investment governance, and large-scale digital transformation

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Chairing IT leadership forums, executive steering committees, and technology governance bodies
- Reviewing and approving major investment decisions, technology programme business cases, and strategic vendor agreements
- Preparing and presenting board papers, risk reports, and technology strategy updates for the CEO and Board
- Engaging with business unit executives to understand strategic priorities and translate them into technology direction
- Conducting strategic 1:1s with Product Area Leads, the TAL, the CISO, and other direct technology leaders
- Reviewing technology portfolio performance: delivery progress, budget adherence, risk posture, and KPIs
- Representing the organisation in external forums, industry bodies, and vendor executive briefings
- Driving the digital transformation agenda through sponsorship, investment decisions, and cultural leadership
- Leading senior technology leadership talent reviews, succession planning, and executive hiring processes
- Evaluating emerging technology trends and setting the organisation's position on innovation adoption

## Key Performance Indicators

- Technology ROI and value realisation from major technology investments
- Digital transformation programme delivery against strategic milestones
- Enterprise technology risk posture (board-level risk rating trend)
- IT spend as a percentage of revenue and against peer benchmarks
- Technology function employee engagement and senior leadership retention
- Board and executive satisfaction with technology governance and reporting
- Strategic vendor and partner performance against enterprise agreements
- Technology capability maturity progression (measured against defined target state)
- Time-to-value for major technology platform and product investments
- Regulatory compliance posture across all technology-governed domains

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; significant on-site presence required for board engagement, executive leadership team participation, major vendor negotiations, and senior leadership forums
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, Power BI, ServiceNow, and executive governance platforms for reporting, planning, and communication
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular presence at headquarters for board meetings, executive team sessions, and key organisational events; travel to major vendor and partner locations as required
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with availability for global vendor briefings, cross-regional executive forums, and board schedule requirements
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Accessible for major technology incidents with board or regulatory implications; provides executive escalation authority during P1 incidents and crisis situations

## Career Development Path

**Previous Roles:**

- VP of Technology or VP of Engineering with enterprise-wide scope
- CTO or CIO in a smaller or less complex organisation, stepping up in scale
- Product Area Lead or Technical Area Lead in a large, complex technology function
- Chief Architect or Group IT Architect with executive leadership development
- Senior IT Director or Head of Technology with a track record of strategic delivery

**Potential Next Roles:**

- Group CTO or Group CIO (larger enterprise or group holding company)
- Non-executive Director (technology-focused board role)
- Chief Executive Officer (technology-led organisation)
- Technology-focused private equity operating partner or venture capital advisor
- Strategic consulting or advisory roles at partner/principal level
