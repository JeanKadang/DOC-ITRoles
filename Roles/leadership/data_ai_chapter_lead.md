## Role Overview

The Data & AI Chapter Lead is the most senior technical manager and people leader for the organisation's data and artificial intelligence capability, spanning four domains: Data Engineering, Data Management, Database Management, and AI Governance. This role combines direct line management of architects and senior engineers across these domains with authoritative ownership of the enterprise data strategy, AI platform governance, data architecture standards, and data governance frameworks. The Chapter Lead is accountable for ensuring data quality, lineage, and accessibility across the estate while overseeing responsible and compliant AI adoption — including the governance of ML model lifecycles and AI ethics policy. Reporting to a Technical Area Lead or Product Area Lead, this role is the definitive "Head of Data & AI" voice below the TAL layer.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Builds and sustains a mature data and AI capability that treats data as a strategic asset — enabling reliable, high-quality, and accessible data for analytics, AI/ML applications, and business intelligence, while ensuring responsible AI adoption and compliance with data governance and ethics obligations
- **Value Metrics:** Data platform adoption and coverage, data quality scores across critical data domains, AI/ML model governance compliance rate, data governance policy adherence, chapter practitioner retention and satisfaction, time-to-access trusted data products
- **Key Stakeholders:** Technical Area Lead / Product Area Lead (line manager), domain architects and senior engineers within the chapter, Enterprise Architect, Chief Data Officer (CDO), CISO and Security & Identity Chapter Lead, Legal and Compliance, AI ethics and risk functions, CTO/CIO
- **Processes Supported:** Enterprise data architecture governance, AI/ML platform strategy and governance, data governance policy framework, data product ownership, AI ethics and compliance review, ML model lifecycle management, data catalogue and lineage management

## Key Responsibilities

- Line manage and develop architects and senior engineers across all four chapter domains, including performance management, career development, and chapter hiring
- Own and maintain the chapter career development framework for data and AI practitioners, running regular 1:1s, development reviews, and talent calibration
- Lead chapter-level hiring: define role standards, conduct or chair technical interviews, and ensure a consistent engineering bar across data engineering, data management, database management, and AI governance domains
- Define and own the enterprise data architecture strategy — governing the data platform, data product framework, and data mesh or centralised architecture patterns used across the organisation
- Lead the AI platform governance framework, overseeing AI/ML platform selection, MLOps practices, model risk management, and responsible AI adoption in alignment with AI ethics policy
- Own the data governance policy framework — defining data quality standards, data lineage requirements, master data management approaches, and data ownership accountability
- Govern the database management strategy — overseeing database platform selection, data tiering, and lifecycle management across transactional, analytical, and operational workloads
- Lead the organisation's AI ethics and compliance programme, working with Legal, Risk, and Compliance functions to ensure AI models and data practices meet regulatory obligations
- Own the chapter roadmap — translating TAL/PAL and CDO strategic direction into chapter-level data and AI technical priorities and delivery commitments
- Represent the chapter in enterprise architecture forums, data governance boards, AI ethics committees, and regulatory compliance discussions

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep technical expertise in data engineering, data architecture, or data platform domains, with strong breadth across data management, database management, and AI/ML governance
- Proven ability to design and govern enterprise data platforms at scale — including data lakes, lakehouses, warehouses, streaming pipelines, and data mesh patterns
- Strong understanding of AI/ML platform architecture, MLOps practices, feature stores, model serving, and ML model risk management
- Experience establishing data governance frameworks: data catalogues, data lineage tools, data quality platforms, and master data management programmes
- Familiarity with data regulatory compliance obligations — GDPR, data residency requirements, AI Act, and responsible AI principles

**Leadership and People Management:**

- Demonstrated line management experience with architect-level or senior engineer practitioners across data and AI disciplines
- Ability to build and sustain high-performing chapters spanning data engineering, data governance, database administration, and AI governance
- Experience designing and running technical hiring processes, including calibration of engineering bars across data-focused domains
- Skilled at engaging Legal, Compliance, and business stakeholders on data governance, AI ethics, and regulatory obligations
- Ability to translate data and AI technical strategy for executive, business, and non-technical audiences including CDO and CTO/CIO levels

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Azure Synapse/Databricks/Snowflake at architecture governance depth
- **Proficient level required:** Data governance platforms (Purview, Collibra, Alation)
- **Working Knowledge required:** ML platforms (Azure ML, SageMaker) at strategic oversight
- **Awareness level expected:** AI ethics and responsible AI frameworks (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act)

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in data architecture, data engineering, data platform, or AI/ML roles
- 3+ years in a Principal Data Architect, Lead Data Engineer, or senior technical leadership role with cross-domain data scope
- Demonstrated experience managing or mentoring architects and senior engineers in a formal or informal leadership capacity
- Track record of owning enterprise data strategy and governance at scale, including data quality programmes and platform consolidation
- Experience leading AI governance or ML risk management programmes in a regulated or enterprise context

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Running chapter 1:1s with domain architects and senior engineers, covering data platform challenges, AI governance decisions, and career development
- Chairing chapter architecture reviews for data platform proposals, AI/ML model deployments, and database architecture decisions
- Engaging with the TAL/PAL and CDO on chapter roadmap priorities, data strategy alignment, and AI governance investment
- Reviewing data quality reports, data lineage coverage, and AI model risk assessments across chapter-owned domains
- Collaborating with the Security & Identity Chapter Lead on data protection and secure data architecture requirements
- Working with Legal and Compliance on AI ethics reviews, data residency obligations, and regulatory data requirements
- Conducting or calibrating technical interviews for data architect, data engineer, and AI governance specialist roles
- Participating in enterprise architecture and data governance forums to represent chapter interests
- Reviewing data product proposals, data catalogue standards, and master data management decisions
- Mentoring domain architects on data strategy, AI platform governance, and technical leadership development

## Key Performance Indicators

- Chapter practitioner retention rate and voluntary attrition trend
- Hiring velocity and quality for chapter architect and senior engineer roles (90-day retention of new hires)
- Chapter satisfaction score (internal survey results for the Data & AI chapter)
- Data platform adoption and coverage across the organisation's data domains
- Data quality scores for critical business data domains
- AI/ML model governance compliance rate — percentage of models with approved governance artefacts
- Data governance policy adherence rate across data-producing teams
- Time-to-access trusted data products for analytics and AI consumers
- Architecture debt reduction trend across data and AI platforms
- AI ethics and compliance review throughput and issue resolution rate

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; people management and chapter leadership responsibilities require regular in-person engagement with practitioners and senior stakeholders
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, GitHub/Azure DevOps, Confluence, Miro, LeanIX, data governance platforms; async-first communication for distributed chapter practitioners
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular on-site presence required for chapter meetings, architecture governance sessions, 1:1s, and cross-chapter leadership forums; on-site presence for major data platform migrations, AI programme launches, and compliance reviews
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional data governance reviews and AI compliance discussions
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Available for escalation on critical data platform or pipeline incidents; participates in post-incident reviews for data platform outages and ML model failures

## Career Development Path

**Previous Roles:**

- Principal Data Architect or Lead Data Engineer with cross-domain data and AI breadth
- Senior AI/ML Architect or Lead ML Engineer with governance and platform leadership experience
- Head of Data Engineering or Data Platform Lead with architecture and people leadership track record
- Domain Architect in data management or database management with strategic scope and leadership experience

**Potential Next Roles:**

- Technical Area Lead (TAL) with data, AI, and analytics scope
- Chief Data Officer (CDO) or Chief AI Officer
- VP of Data Engineering or VP of AI Platform
- CTO (particularly for data-led or AI-native organisations)
