## Role Overview

The Security & Identity Chapter Lead is the organisation's most senior security practitioner and people leader below the CISO layer, spanning five domains: Security, Security Cross-Platform, Security & Identity, Data Protection, and Directory Services. This role combines direct line management of security architects and senior engineers with authoritative ownership of the security architecture strategy, identity governance framework, zero trust implementation roadmap, and the organisation's compliance posture. The Chapter Lead is the practitioner-level technical authority for security across the entire technology estate — ensuring security architecture standards, identity platform strategy, data protection policy, and threat modelling standards are coherent, current, and enforced. Reporting to a Technical Area Lead or Product Area Lead, this role works in close partnership with the CISO and is the definitive "Head of Security Architecture" voice for the engineering organisation.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the organisation's technology estate is secured by design — through coherent security architecture standards, a mature identity governance framework, a defined zero trust roadmap, and a security compliance programme that reduces risk and meets regulatory obligations across all platforms
- **Value Metrics:** Security architecture standards adoption rate, zero trust roadmap milestone completion, identity governance coverage, data protection policy compliance rate, security toolchain rationalisation progress, chapter practitioner retention, mean time to detect/respond to security incidents escalated from architecture gaps
- **Key Stakeholders:** Technical Area Lead / Product Area Lead (line manager), CISO and Head of Security, domain architects and senior engineers within the chapter, Enterprise Architect, Legal and Compliance, Data Protection Officer (DPO), Risk function, CTO/CIO
- **Processes Supported:** Security architecture governance and review, zero trust implementation programme, identity platform strategy and governance, data protection policy framework, threat modelling process, security toolchain governance, security compliance reporting, vulnerability and risk remediation

## Key Responsibilities

- Line manage and develop security architects and senior engineers across all five chapter domains, including performance management, career development, and chapter hiring
- Own and maintain the chapter career development framework for security and identity practitioners, running regular 1:1s, development reviews, and talent calibration
- Lead chapter-level hiring: define role standards, conduct or chair technical interviews, and ensure a consistent security engineering bar across all five domains
- Own the security architecture standards framework — defining design patterns, security controls, and architecture review criteria that all technology platforms must comply with
- Drive the organisation's zero trust implementation roadmap, overseeing architecture, sequencing, and delivery across identity, network, endpoint, and application security dimensions
- Lead the identity platform strategy — governing Entra ID (Azure AD), Privileged Access Management (PAM), Identity Governance & Administration (IGA), and directory services architecture
- Own the data protection policy from a technical architecture perspective — including encryption standards, data classification controls, DLP architecture, and privacy-by-design requirements
- Define and maintain threat modelling standards, ensuring security risk assessment is embedded in the architecture review process across all chapters
- Lead security toolchain governance — rationalising and standardising the security tooling portfolio (SIEM, EDR, CSPM, CNAPP, vulnerability management) across the estate
- Represent the chapter in enterprise architecture forums, security governance bodies, risk committees, and regulatory compliance discussions

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Security architecture standards, security design patterns, and the chapter-wide security controls framework | Enterprise security strategy, risk appetite, and security programme investment (CISO-owned) |
| Zero trust roadmap architecture and implementation standards across identity, network, endpoint, and application layers | Programme funding, sequencing, and organisational risk acceptance decisions (CISO and TAL/PAL-owned) |

## Required Skills

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep technical expertise across at least two security domains (e.g., identity and access management, cloud security, data protection) with strong breadth across the full five-domain chapter scope
- Proven ability to design and govern security architecture frameworks at enterprise scale, including zero trust architecture, cloud security posture management, and identity governance
- Strong understanding of identity and access management platforms: Entra ID (Azure AD), Active Directory, PAM solutions (CyberArk, BeyondTrust), and IGA platforms (SailPoint, Saviynt)
- Experience establishing data protection architecture: encryption standards, DLP controls, data classification frameworks, and privacy-by-design patterns
- Deep familiarity with security compliance frameworks: ISO 27001, NIST CSF, IEC 62443, CIS Controls, SOC 2, Cyber Essentials Plus, GDPR, and NIS2

**Leadership and People Management:**

- Demonstrated line management experience with security architect-level or senior engineer practitioners across multiple security disciplines
- Ability to build and sustain high-performing security chapters spanning diverse specialisms including identity, cloud security, data protection, and directory services
- Experience designing and running technical hiring processes, including calibration of security engineering bars across specialised domains
- Skilled at engaging CISO, Legal, Risk, and Compliance stakeholders on security architecture, threat posture, and regulatory obligations
- Ability to translate complex security architecture decisions and risk implications for executive and non-technical audiences

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Microsoft Sentinel/Defender at CSPM governance depth
- **Proficient level required:** Microsoft Entra ID / Azure AD at IAM governance depth
- **Working Knowledge required:** Zero trust architecture frameworks and tooling
- **Awareness level expected:** GRC platforms (ServiceNow GRC, Archer) for risk oversight

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in security architecture, identity and access management, or senior security engineering roles
- 3+ years in a Principal Security Architect, Lead Security Architect, or senior security leadership role with cross-domain scope
- Demonstrated experience managing or mentoring security architects and senior engineers in a formal or informal leadership capacity
- Track record of owning security architecture governance, zero trust programme delivery, or identity platform strategy at enterprise scale
- Deep familiarity with security compliance and regulatory frameworks applicable to the organisation's industry context

- Running chapter 1:1s with security architects and senior engineers, covering security design challenges, incident learnings, career development, and domain governance
- Chairing chapter security architecture review boards and reviewing significant security design proposals across all technology domains
- Working with the CISO on zero trust programme progress, security strategy alignment, and escalated security risk decisions
- Engaging with other Chapter Leads to embed security requirements and threat modelling into their architecture review processes

- Mentoring domain architects on security strategy, zero trust architecture, and technical leadership development

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Hybrid; the sensitive nature of security responsibilities and people management obligations require regular in-person engagement with the chapter, CISO, and senior stakeholders
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, GitHub/Azure DevOps, Confluence, Miro; security-cleared or restricted channels for sensitive security discussions; compliance and GRC platforms for governance reporting
- **On-Site Requirements:** Regular on-site presence required for chapter meetings, security architecture reviews, CISO engagement, 1:1s with practitioners, and compliance or audit activities; on-site presence mandatory for sensitive incident response or regulatory reviews
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Standard business hours with flexibility for cross-regional security governance and incident escalation
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Available for escalation on critical security incidents and architecture-level vulnerability disclosures; participates in major security incident post-incident reviews and systemic remediation planning; may be required to support out-of-hours response for significant security events
