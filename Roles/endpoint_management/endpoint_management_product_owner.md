## Role Overview

The Endpoint Management Product Owner (PO) owns the product vision, roadmap, and backlog for the organisation's enterprise endpoint management platform. This role bridges business requirements - security, compliance, workforce productivity, and cost - with the engineering team's delivery capacity. The PO prioritises work across the endpoint estate (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android) and ensures the platform evolves to meet workforce and security needs while delivering measurable business value.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — endpoint management backlog, roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Technical implementation approach (Architect/Senior Engineer-owned); security policy design (with CISO); licensing commercial negotiations (with Procurement); organisational onboarding and change management (with HR)
- **Escalates To:** End User & Workplace Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Endpoint Management Architect on roadmap and architecture-alignment questions

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensure the endpoint management platform delivers a secure, compliant, and productive device experience across the organisation, aligned with workforce and security strategy.
- **Value Metrics:** Device compliance rate, time-to-compliance for new security requirements, Autopilot provisioning lead time, platform backlog velocity, cost per managed device.
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, IT Operations leadership, HR, Help Desk, Finance (licensing), Compliance and Risk teams, End User community.
- **Processes Supported:** Employee onboarding, device provisioning, patch lifecycle, application delivery, security compliance enforcement, regulatory audit readiness.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and maintain the endpoint management product backlog, ensuring it reflects security, compliance, operational, and user experience priorities.
- Define and communicate the platform roadmap, aligning endpoint capabilities with the organisation's hybrid and remote workforce strategy.
- Write and refine user stories and acceptance criteria for endpoint platform capabilities.
- Collaborate with the CISO and security teams to prioritise security baseline and Conditional Access initiatives.
- Manage stakeholder expectations and provide regular status reporting on platform health and roadmap progress.
- Own the endpoint management tooling licensing strategy, including Microsoft Intune, MECM, and related Microsoft 365 components.
- Evaluate new Microsoft Intune capabilities and Autopatch features, working with the architect to define adoption strategies.
- Represent endpoint management in enterprise architecture forums and cross-domain planning sessions.
- Define success metrics for the endpoint platform and report against KPIs to leadership.
- Drive prioritisation of co-management migration from SCCM to cloud-native Intune delivery.

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Working knowledge of Microsoft Intune, MECM, and modern endpoint management concepts (sufficient to facilitate technical discussions and evaluate feasibility).
- Understanding of device compliance, Conditional Access, and Zero Trust endpoint concepts.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum backlog management and user story writing.
- Experience with ITSM tooling (ServiceNow, Jira) for backlog and request management.
- Ability to interpret endpoint compliance dashboards and reporting metrics.

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Strong stakeholder management and communication across technical and business audiences.
- Ability to balance security requirements, operational feasibility, and user experience when prioritising.
- Skilled at translating business requirements into actionable engineering work.
- Confident facilitating workshops, backlog refinement sessions, and roadmap reviews.
