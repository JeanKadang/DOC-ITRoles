## Role Overview

The Data Engineering Product Owner owns the vision, roadmap, and delivery backlog for the organisation's data platform and data engineering capabilities. This role ensures the data platform investment delivers measurable business value - enabling analytics, BI, and ML use cases to be powered by reliable, timely, and trustworthy data. The Data Engineering PO bridges the CDO's data strategy with the technical delivery of the data engineering team, prioritising work that maximises data-driven business outcomes.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — data engineering backlog, delivery prioritisation, and roadmap alignment to enterprise data strategy
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Data platform architecture and technical constraints (Data Platform Architect-owned); pipeline reliability acceptance criteria detail (DataOps Specialists-owned); enterprise data strategy setting (CDO-owned, this role translates it into deliverables)
- **Escalates To:** Data & AI Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** DataOps Specialists on acceptance criteria for pipeline reliability and CI/CD work

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensure the data platform enables the organisation's analytics, BI, and ML capabilities through reliable, governed, and business-aligned data product delivery.
- **Value Metrics:** Data product adoption by analytics and ML consumers, time-to-production for new data products, data platform cost per TB, data quality coverage rate, stakeholder satisfaction with data availability and trustworthiness.
- **Key Stakeholders:** Chief Data Officer (CDO), Analytics and BI leads, Data Science teams, Finance, Compliance, IT Operations, Data Governance.
- **Processes Supported:** Data product delivery, data platform evolution, data governance implementation, BI and reporting enablement, ML feature pipeline delivery.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own and prioritise the data engineering team's backlog, ensuring it reflects CDO strategy, analytics requirements, and platform reliability improvements.
- Define and communicate the data platform roadmap: technology evolution, new data domain delivery, and governance maturity milestones.
- Collaborate with analytics leads, data scientists, and product owners to capture and prioritise data requirements.
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria for data pipeline, data model, and platform capability work.
- Own the data platform technology licensing strategy (Databricks, Snowflake, dbt Cloud) and renewal planning.
- Define data product acceptance criteria with data consumers: freshness SLAs, quality thresholds, and schema contracts.
- Represent the data engineering team in enterprise architecture, data governance, and IT portfolio forums.
- Drive data platform FinOps: monitor platform costs, prioritise cost optimisation initiatives, and report to Finance.
- Evaluate new data platform capabilities and define adoption roadmap in collaboration with the Data Platform Architect.
- Report on data platform KPIs and delivery metrics to CDO and IT leadership.

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Sufficient data engineering knowledge to evaluate feasibility and facilitate technical trade-off discussions.
- Understanding of data platform concepts: data lakehouse, ELT/ETL, orchestration, data quality, and streaming.
- Familiarity with common data platform tools: Databricks, Snowflake, dbt, Airflow.
- Agile/Scrum product ownership experience with backlog management tools (Jira, Azure DevOps).
- Ability to read data quality dashboards and platform cost reports.
- Understanding of data governance concepts: data catalogue, lineage, data classification.

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Ability to translate complex data requirements into clear, actionable engineering work.
- Skilled stakeholder facilitator across business sponsors, data scientists, and engineering teams.
- Outcome-oriented mindset: focused on data product adoption and trust, not pipeline count.
- Confident in data platform FinOps conversations with Finance teams.

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Data engineering backlog and sprint management (Jira, Azure DevOps), data platform roadmap governance and CDO stakeholder communication, data product SLA and quality threshold definition
- **Proficient level required:** Databricks/Snowflake/BigQuery (platform familiarity for FinOps and adoption decisions), dbt Cloud usage dashboards and adoption metrics, data quality and observability tool reporting (Monte Carlo, Soda)
- **Working Knowledge required:** Data catalogue tools (Databricks Unity Catalog, Collibra, DataHub — governance reporting level), data governance concepts (lineage, classification, data mesh), Power BI/Tableau for platform analytics
- **Awareness level expected:** MLOps and AI feature pipeline concepts, streaming data platform concepts (Kafka, Kinesis), emerging data lakehouse technologies (Apache Iceberg, Apache Hudi)

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Running sprint planning and backlog refinement with the data engineering team.
- Meeting with analytics leads and data scientists to gather and prioritise data requirements.
- Reviewing data platform cost reports and initiating FinOps optimisation items.
- Evaluating Databricks/Snowflake release notes for adoption planning.
- Presenting data platform roadmap and delivery metrics to CDO and stakeholders.
- Writing acceptance criteria for new data product backlog items.
- Facilitating data SLA definition workshops with data consumers.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Data product delivery velocity (sprint throughput)
- Data product adoption rate (active consumers by product)
- Data platform cost per TB processed (trend: optimising)
- Data pipeline availability SLA compliance
- Stakeholder satisfaction score with data platform
- Roadmap milestone delivery against plan
