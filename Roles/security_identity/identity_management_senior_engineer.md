## Role Overview

The Identity Management Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of enterprise identity management solutions. This role provides technical leadership for identity systems while working closely with architects to translate identity strategies into effective implementations.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — advanced identity solution design and delivery within the Identity Management Architect's reference architecture
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in identity engineering with demonstrated independent delivery — operates independently within the Architect's reference architecture
- **Out of Scope:** Identity management architecture and standards (Architect-owned); access management (authorization) implementation (Access Management Senior Engineers-owned, this role integrates with it); cloud identity service selection (Cloud Senior Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it)
- **Escalates To:** Identity Management Architect — solution design and implementation strategy exceptions
- **Escalated To By:** Identity Management Engineers on technical implementation issues

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Implements and optimizes enterprise identity management solutions ensuring reliable, secure authentication and efficient identity lifecycle management for the entire workforce
- **Value Metrics:** Identity provisioning and deprovisioning cycle time, authentication service availability, lifecycle automation coverage percentage, reduction in identity-related security incidents
- **Key Stakeholders:** Identity Management Architect, CISO team, HR, application architects, Access Management Senior Engineers
- **Processes Supported:** Employee identity lifecycle management, authentication service delivery, hybrid identity and federation configuration, identity governance implementation

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex identity management solutions based on architectural guidance
- Develop automation for identity provisioning and lifecycle management
- Lead troubleshooting for critical identity management issues
- Create and maintain identity management standards and procedures
- Implement advanced authentication and federation solutions
- Design directory services and identity repository architectures
- Develop custom identity integrations and connectors
- Provide technical mentorship to Identity Management Engineers
