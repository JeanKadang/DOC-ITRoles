## Role Overview

The Access Management Engineer implements and maintains access management systems across the organization. Working with the Access Management Architect and Product Owner, this role ensures secure, efficient, and appropriate access to systems and data for users and applications.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Team — execution of access provisioning and control configuration tasks to defined standards
- **Experience Anchor:** 1-3 years in access management or identity engineering — works under guidance, building toward independent delivery
- **Out of Scope:** Access management architecture and solution design (Senior Engineers and the Architect-owned); identity (authentication) provisioning (Identity Management Engineers-owned, this role integrates with it); broader security control implementation (Security Engineers-owned, this role coordinates with it)
- **Escalates To:** Access Management Senior Engineer — design-level questions and complex implementation issues
- **Escalated To By:** application teams on access requirements

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Operates access management systems that enforce security policies and ensure users have appropriate, timely access to perform their roles effectively across the organization
- **Value Metrics:** Access request fulfillment time, account provisioning and deprovisioning accuracy, access certification completion rates, reduction in access-related helpdesk tickets
- **Key Stakeholders:** Access Management Senior Engineer, HR, application owners, service desk, end users requiring access support
- **Processes Supported:** Employee onboarding and offboarding access setup, access request workflows, periodic access certification execution, password management and MFA administration

## Key Responsibilities

- Deploy and configure access management solutions
- Implement access policies and role definitions
- Configure access request and approval workflows
- Set up access certification campaigns
- Troubleshoot and resolve access-related incidents
- Implement access management integrations with enterprise applications
- Generate access reports and audit data
- Document access management configurations and procedures

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Day-to-day configuration and operation of access management platforms and tools | Technical implementation approach and solution architecture decisions |
| Access request fulfillment, role assignments, and certification campaign execution | Access governance policy and role taxonomy design |
| Incident troubleshooting and resolution for access-related issues and outages | Platform selection and access management architecture decisions |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Experience with access management tools and platforms
- Knowledge of access control concepts (RBAC, ABAC)
- Understanding of access management protocols and standards
- Familiarity with privileged access management tools
- Basic scripting skills for access automation
- Understanding of access governance concepts
- Knowledge of access security principles
- Relevant certifications in access management or security

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates access-related issues and resolution progress clearly to end users and technical stakeholders throughout the incident lifecycle
- Works effectively with HR, application teams, and security engineers to fulfill access requests accurately and resolve conflicts promptly
- Methodically diagnoses access issues using systematic troubleshooting techniques to restore normal operations with minimal business disruption
