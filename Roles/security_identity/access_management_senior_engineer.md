## Role Overview

The Access Management Senior Engineer leads the implementation and optimization of enterprise access management solutions. This role provides technical leadership for access control systems while working closely with architects to translate access strategies into effective implementations.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Leads implementation and optimization of access management controls that protect sensitive systems while enabling efficient, governed access for business operations
- **Value Metrics:** Reduction in access provisioning cycle time, privileged access session audit rates, access certification completion rates, decrease in orphaned and over-privileged accounts
- **Key Stakeholders:** Access Management Architect, CISO team, application owners, HR, compliance and audit teams
- **Processes Supported:** Role-based access provisioning, privileged access workflows, access governance campaigns, incident response for access-related security events

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement complex access management solutions based on architectural guidance
- Develop automation for access provisioning and governance processes
- Lead troubleshooting for critical access management issues
- Create and maintain access management standards and procedures
- Implement advanced access control models and strategies
- Design privileged access management solutions for sensitive systems
- Develop custom access management integrations and extensions
- Provide technical mentorship to Access Management Engineers

## Key Decisions and Accountabilities

| Owns | Advises On |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Extensive experience with enterprise access management solutions
- Advanced knowledge of access control models and frameworks
- Strong understanding of access governance best practices
- Experience with access management automation and scripting
- Deep knowledge of privileged access management techniques
- Advanced troubleshooting skills for complex access issues
- Experience with regulatory compliance related to access control
- Relevant advanced certifications in security or access management

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Communicates complex access management concepts and implementation trade-offs clearly to both technical engineers and non-technical stakeholders
- Partners effectively with security, HR, and application teams to deliver integrated access governance solutions and cross-functional access campaigns
- Diagnoses and resolves complex access and governance issues through methodical root cause analysis and systematic troubleshooting

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Microsoft Entra ID (advanced configurations), CyberArk/BeyondTrust (PAM), access governance/certification tools
- **Proficient level required:** OAuth 2.0/OIDC/SAML, automated access provisioning, risk-based access control (ABAC)
- **Working Knowledge required:** Access management APIs/integrations, PowerShell/Python automation
- **Awareness level expected:** SailPoint/Saviynt (IGA), AI-driven access analytics

## Remote Work Considerations

- **Remote Eligibility:** Fully remote eligible; access management engineering work is performed through secure remote access to identity platforms
- **Collaboration Tools:** Microsoft Teams, Jira, Confluence, GitHub/ADO, SailPoint and CyberArk admin portals, PowerShell and scripting environments
- **On-Site Requirements:** Minimal; occasional on-site access for hardware-bound integrations or sensitive PAM system administration in secure zones
- **Time Zone Flexibility:** Core business hours alignment expected; some flexibility for cross-team collaboration spanning multiple regions
- **On-Call / Operational Demands:** Participates in on-call rotation for access management system incidents covering authentication failures, PAM outages, and critical provisioning errors
