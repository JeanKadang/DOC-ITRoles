## Role Overview

The Access Management Product Owner manages the development and lifecycle of the organization's access management solutions. This role leads a team of access management architects and engineers, ensuring secure and efficient access to systems and data while meeting business requirements and compliance standards.

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures the access management portfolio delivers secure, compliant, and efficient access services that reduce organizational risk while supporting business productivity and workforce agility
- **Value Metrics:** Access request cycle time reduction, certification campaign completion rates, privileged access security coverage, reduction in access-related incidents, stakeholder satisfaction scores
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, HR leadership, Risk and Compliance teams, application owners, business unit leaders, IT leadership
- **Processes Supported:** Workforce access lifecycle management, access governance and compliance reporting, privileged access oversight, self-service access request capabilities

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for access management solutions
- Prioritize access management initiatives based on risk assessment and business impact
- Define acceptance criteria for access management deliverables
- Lead sprint planning and agile ceremonies for the access management team
- Engage with business stakeholders to understand access requirements
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for access management technologies
- Balance security requirements with user experience considerations
- Track and report on access management metrics and effectiveness
- Ensure access governance and compliance
- Implement privileged access management
- Optimize user access experience and efficiency
- Improve access request and approval workflows
- Conduct access certification and reviews
- Modernize access management technology
- Develop self-service access capabilities
- Enhance access management reporting and analytics

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Strong understanding of access management concepts and technologies
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product ownership
- Background in access management or identity and access management
- Knowledge of security and compliance requirements
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
- Analytical approach to risk assessment and access governance
- Experience with enterprise access management solutions
- Understanding of security operations and procedures

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Translates complex access security and governance requirements into clear business value narratives for executive and non-technical stakeholders
- Bridges security, compliance, HR, and application teams to build shared understanding and consensus on access management priorities and delivery plans
- Balances competing security requirements, user experience expectations, and compliance obligations through structured prioritization and risk-based analysis

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** Microsoft Entra ID, ServiceNow (access request workflows), Jira/Azure DevOps
- **Proficient level required:** CyberArk/BeyondTrust (PAM), Power BI (access reporting), access certification platforms
- **Working Knowledge required:** SailPoint/Saviynt (IGA), Azure MFA/SSO
- **Awareness level expected:** AI-driven access governance, Zero Trust access frameworks
