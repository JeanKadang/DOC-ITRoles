## Role Overview

The Access Management Architect designs and oversees the organization's access management strategy and solutions. This role ensures secure, efficient, and appropriate access to systems and data while maintaining compliance with security policies and regulatory requirements.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — access management (authorization) architecture, RBAC/ABAC models, and access governance standards across the chapter
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in access management or identity architecture with demonstrated architecture-level delivery — operates independently on domain-wide access architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Identity (authentication) management architecture (Identity Management Architect-owned, this role integrates with it); access monitoring tooling design (Observability Architect-owned, this role consumes it); enterprise access governance policy (compliance and risk management-owned, this role implements it)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities and cross-domain investment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** Access Management Senior Engineers on solution design and implementation strategy

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs access management controls that protect sensitive systems and data while enabling appropriate, efficient access that supports business operations and Zero Trust security objectives
- **Value Metrics:** Reduction in access-related security incidents, access governance compliance rates, privileged access coverage, access certification efficiency improvements
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, Risk and Compliance teams, HR, application owners, Cloud Platform Architects, business unit leaders
- **Processes Supported:** Employee access lifecycle, privileged access management workflows, access certification campaigns, Zero Trust access enforcement, compliance auditing

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and architect comprehensive access management solutions
- Develop access governance frameworks and control structures
- Create privileged access management strategies and architectures
- Design access management integration with enterprise applications
- Develop access certification and attestation processes
- Create access reporting and analytics frameworks
- Evaluate and recommend access management technologies
- Provide technical leadership for access management initiatives

## Required Skills & Qualifications

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep knowledge of access management principles and technologies
- Experience with role-based access control (RBAC) and attribute-based access control (ABAC)
- Understanding of privileged access management (PAM) concepts
- Knowledge of identity governance and administration principles
- Familiarity with access management standards and protocols (OAuth, SAML, etc.)
- Experience with access management tools and platforms
- Understanding of security and compliance requirements
- Relevant certifications (CISSP, CISM, vendor certifications)

**Soft Skills and Leadership:**

- Effectively communicates access risk, governance requirements, and architecture trade-offs to executive and technical stakeholders
- Collaborates across security, HR, compliance, and application teams to build comprehensive and enforceable access governance frameworks
- Applies structured analysis to balance stringent security requirements with operational access needs and end-user experience

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

- **Expert level required:** SailPoint/Saviynt (IGA), CyberArk/BeyondTrust (PAM), Microsoft Entra ID (Zero Trust access)
- **Proficient level required:** OAuth 2.0/OIDC/SAML, Cloud Access Security Broker (CASB), RBAC/ABAC frameworks
- **Working Knowledge required:** API/service access management, access analytics platforms
- **Awareness level expected:** AI-driven access governance, SSPM (SaaS security posture management)
