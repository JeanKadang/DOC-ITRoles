## Role Overview

The Identity Management Architect designs and oversees the organization's identity management strategy and solutions. This role focuses on creating a secure, scalable, and efficient identity infrastructure that supports authentication, lifecycle management, and identity governance across the enterprise.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain-wide — identity management architecture, directory services strategy, and hybrid identity standards across the chapter
- **Experience Anchor:** 8+ years in identity architecture with demonstrated architecture-level delivery — operates independently on domain-wide identity architecture decisions
- **Out of Scope:** Access management (authorization) architecture (Access Management Architect-owned, this role integrates identity with it); Active Directory infrastructure operations (Windows Server Architect-owned, this role aligns to it); application-level identity integration detail (application architects-owned, this role provides standards to them)
- **Escalates To:** Security & Identity Chapter Lead — chapter-wide priorities and cross-domain investment decisions
- **Escalated To By:** Identity Management Senior Engineers on solution design and implementation strategy

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Designs the identity management infrastructure that underpins workforce authentication and lifecycle management, enabling secure access across enterprise systems and supporting Zero Trust security objectives
- **Value Metrics:** Identity system availability and authentication success rates, lifecycle automation coverage, reduction in identity-related security incidents, IGA compliance scores
- **Key Stakeholders:** CISO, Enterprise Architects, HR leadership, Cloud Platform Architects, application owners, Access Management Architect
- **Processes Supported:** Employee identity lifecycle (joiner/mover/leaver), enterprise authentication and SSO, federation and directory architecture, identity governance and compliance

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and architect comprehensive identity management solutions
- Develop identity governance frameworks and processes
- Create authentication and federation architectures
- Design identity lifecycle management processes
- Develop identity repository and directory service strategies
- Create identity integration approaches for enterprise applications
- Evaluate and recommend identity management technologies
- Provide technical leadership for identity management initiatives

**Technical Skills:**

- Deep knowledge of identity management principles and technologies
- Experience with directory services and identity repositories
- Understanding of authentication protocols and standards (SAML, OAuth, OIDC)
- Knowledge of identity governance and administration (IGA) concepts
- Familiarity with federated identity management
- Experience with identity management tools and platforms
