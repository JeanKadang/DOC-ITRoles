## Role Overview

The AWS Cloud Platform Product Owner manages the organization's Amazon Web Services portfolio and adoption roadmap. This role leads a team of AWS architects and engineers, ensuring that AWS cloud services deliver maximum business value while maintaining security, compliance, and cost efficiency.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Domain — AWS platform backlog, investment roadmap, and delivery prioritisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 5+ years in product ownership or technical product management — operates independently on backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Out of Scope:** AWS technical architecture (AWS Cloud Platform Architect-owned); underlying database service strategy (Database Product Owner-owned, this role aligns to it); AWS control implementation detail (Security and Compliance teams-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead — budget, headcount, and cross-domain roadmap trade-offs
- **Escalated To By:** Application Product Owners on platform requirements

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Maximizes the business value delivered by the AWS cloud platform through strategic prioritization of cloud capabilities, governed cloud operations, and cost-efficient infrastructure delivery
- **Value Metrics:** Cloud cost savings against on-premises baseline, time-to-delivery for cloud services, platform availability scores, cloud adoption rates across business units
- **Key Stakeholders:** CTO/CIO, business unit leaders, FinOps team, application product owners, Security and Compliance teams
- **Processes Supported:** Cloud platform service delivery, AWS account governance, cloud cost management (FinOps), cloud adoption and migration programs, cloud security posture reporting

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the product backlog for AWS cloud platform services
- Prioritize AWS initiatives based on business impact and strategic alignment
- Define acceptance criteria for AWS platform capabilities and features
- Facilitate agile processes for the AWS platform team
- Engage with business stakeholders to identify cloud adoption opportunities
- Create and maintain a strategic roadmap for AWS usage and optimization
- Oversee AWS account structure and governance
- Track and optimize cloud spending through FinOps practices

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| AWS platform product backlog prioritization and strategic cloud adoption roadmap | Cloud architecture strategy and AWS service technology selection |
| Acceptance criteria and delivery scope for AWS platform capabilities | Enterprise cloud security policy and compliance requirements |
| Cloud cost governance priorities and FinOps investment decisions | Application migration sequencing and business unit engagement strategy |

**Awareness level expected:**

- AWS AI/ML services (Bedrock, SageMaker) for evaluating future platform capability roadmap items
