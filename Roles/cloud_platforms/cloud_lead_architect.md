## Role Overview

The Cloud Lead Architect provides technical leadership across a cluster of cloud platform architects (Azure, AWS, GCP). This role bridges the gap between the cross-organizational cloud strategy set by the Principal Cloud Architect and the platform-specific designs delivered by individual platform architects. The Cloud Lead Architect ensures architectural coherence, governs design quality, and acts as the primary escalation point for complex cross-platform cloud problems.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Cross-platform — architectural coherence and design quality across the AWS, Azure, and GCP Cloud Architects
- **Experience Anchor:** 10+ years in cloud architecture with demonstrated cross-platform technical leadership — bridges the Principal Cloud Architect's strategy and the platform architects' designs, acting as primary escalation point for complex cross-platform problems
- **Out of Scope:** Multi-year multi-cloud strategy setting (Cloud Principal Architect-owned, this role executes the platform-level guidance it translates); platform-specific implementation detail (AWS/Azure/GCP Cloud Architects-owned); formal line management of the platform architects (Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Cloud Principal Architect — cross-organisational strategy questions
- **Escalated To By:** AWS, Azure, and GCP Cloud Architects on complex cross-platform cloud problems

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Ensures that cloud architectures delivered across all cloud platforms are consistent, secure, cost-effective, and aligned with organizational standards — reducing risk and accelerating delivery by establishing shared patterns and practices
- **Value Metrics:** Cross-platform architecture consistency score, reduction in platform-specific technical debt, architect team delivery velocity, time-to-resolve architectural escalations, adoption rate of shared reference patterns
- **Key Stakeholders:** TAL (Technical Area Lead), Principal Cloud Architect, Azure/AWS/GCP Cloud Architects, Security and Compliance, FinOps, Engineering Leadership
- **Processes Supported:** Architecture governance and review, cloud platform standards definition, cross-platform design consistency, architect mentorship and quality assurance

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead and coordinate the work of Azure, AWS, and GCP Cloud Architects within the cloud domain
- Own the cloud architecture review and approval process across all platforms
- Define and maintain shared architecture standards, reference patterns, and guardrails used by all platform architects
- Identify architectural inconsistencies or gaps across cloud platforms and drive resolution
- Act as the primary escalation point for complex architectural decisions that span multiple cloud platforms
- Represent cloud architecture in cross-domain technical forums and steering groups
- Mentor and develop platform architects, raising architectural quality and consistency
- Translate Principal Cloud Architect strategy into actionable platform-specific guidance and roadmaps
- Contribute to the multi-cloud strategy by providing deep implementation insight to inform higher-level direction

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Cloud architecture review and approval process; design quality gates across all cloud platforms | Multi-cloud strategy direction (owned by Principal Cloud Architect) |
| Shared reference architectures, cross-platform guardrails, and design pattern standards | Enterprise-wide architecture standards (owned by Enterprise Architecture domain) |
| Escalated architectural decisions spanning more than one cloud platform | Platform-specific landing zone design (owned by platform architects) |
| Cloud architect team priorities and workload coordination | Cloud platform roadmap investment and procurement decisions |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Expert-level knowledge across at least two of Azure, AWS, and GCP architectural patterns and services
- Demonstrated experience leading a team of architects or senior technical professionals
- Strong ability to identify and resolve architectural inconsistencies across different technology platforms
- Experience defining and enforcing architecture governance processes at scale
- Deep understanding of cloud security, networking, identity, and cost governance across multiple providers
- Ability to translate high-level strategy into actionable technical standards and deliverables
- Strong communication skills for presenting architectural decisions to senior technical and business stakeholders
- Experience with enterprise cloud adoption frameworks (CAF, WAF, AWS Well-Architected, GCP Architecture Framework)

## Typical Day-to-Day Activities

- Conducting and chairing cloud architecture review boards for cross-platform designs
- Reviewing platform architect deliverables and providing structured feedback
- Updating and publishing shared cloud architecture standards and reference patterns
- Resolving cross-platform architectural escalations from engineering teams
- Participating in enterprise architecture steering meetings representing the cloud domain
- Coaching platform architects on design quality, consistency, and governance
- Aligning with the Principal Cloud Architect on strategy implementation progress
- Reviewing and approving major IaC module updates that affect shared cloud foundations

## Key Performance Indicators

- Architecture review cycle time and quality of approved designs
- Adoption rate of shared reference architectures across cloud platforms
- Number of cross-platform architectural inconsistencies identified and resolved
- Architect team satisfaction and professional development progress
- Reduction in escalated architectural issues reaching the Principal Cloud Architect
- Cross-platform cloud governance compliance rate
- Contribution to and maintenance of shared architectural standards library
