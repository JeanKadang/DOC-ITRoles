## Role Overview

The Cloud Principal Architect is the organization's foremost individual contributor for cloud technology strategy. Operating below the Technical Area Lead (TAL), this role shapes the multi-year, multi-cloud architecture vision and translates it into a coherent strategy that the Lead Cloud Architect and platform architects execute against. The Principal Cloud Architect sets the standard for how cloud technology is adopted, governed, and evolved across the organization — influencing decisions at the intersection of business strategy, engineering capability, and cloud innovation.

## Role Scope & Boundaries

- **Scope of Influence:** Enterprise-wide — multi-year, multi-cloud architecture strategy and standards across the organisation
- **Experience Anchor:** 12+ years in cloud architecture with demonstrated organisation-wide strategic ownership — operates independently below the TAL, shaping strategy the Cloud Lead Architect and platform architects execute against
- **Out of Scope:** Day-to-day platform-specific architecture decisions (Cloud Lead Architect and platform Architects-owned); chapter-level people management of domain architects and senior engineers (Cloud, Platform & Infrastructure Chapter Lead-owned); business unit budget allocation (PAL/Finance-owned)
- **Escalates To:** Technical Area Lead (TAL) — strategic direction and cross-chapter alignment
- **Escalated To By:** Cloud Lead Architect on cross-platform strategy questions and architecture governance escalations

## Business Impact

- **Business Objective:** Defines and owns the organization's multi-cloud architecture strategy, ensuring cloud investments are aligned with long-term business objectives, industry best practices, and technology innovation — directly reducing strategic risk and enabling competitive advantage through cloud
- **Value Metrics:** Multi-cloud strategy coverage and maturity, reduction in costly architectural re-work, cloud platform adoption rate against roadmap, number of strategic cloud initiatives shaped or led, external recognition or benchmark positioning
- **Key Stakeholders:** TAL, PAL, CTO/CIO, Lead Cloud Architect, Security and Compliance leadership, FinOps leadership, enterprise cloud service providers (Microsoft, AWS, Google)
- **Processes Supported:** Multi-cloud strategy development and governance, cloud investment planning, architecture standards and policy ownership, cloud innovation scouting and adoption, executive stakeholder advisory

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and maintain the organization's multi-cloud architecture strategy, encompassing Azure, AWS, and GCP
- Set the long-term technical vision for cloud platform adoption, evolution, and retirement
- Establish the architectural principles, standards, and guardrails that govern all cloud domains
- Identify and assess emerging cloud technologies, recommending adoption or rejection with strategic justification
- Drive architectural decisions for the organization's most complex, high-risk, or strategically significant cloud programs
- Engage directly with executive stakeholders and cloud providers as the organization's senior technical cloud authority
- Provide architectural oversight to the Lead Cloud Architect, ensuring strategy is coherently translated into platform-level standards
- Represent the cloud architecture practice in cross-domain enterprise architecture governance
- Act as final escalation point for architectural decisions that cannot be resolved at platform or lead level
- Contribute to and influence industry communities, cloud provider advisory programs, and internal innovation initiatives

## Key Decisions & Accountabilities

> Clarifies what this role **owns** (decides independently) vs. **advises on** (input without final authority).

| Owns | Advises On |
|---|---|
| Multi-cloud architecture strategy, principles, and long-term vision | Overall IT strategy and portfolio investment (owned by TAL and PAL) |
| Cloud architectural standards framework: policies, guardrails, and reference architecture mandate | Organizational operating model and team structure decisions |
| Evaluation and adoption/rejection decisions for strategic cloud services and platforms | Enterprise architecture governance (influences, but does not own) |
| Escalated architectural decisions at the highest level of cloud complexity or organizational risk | Business case approval for cloud investment programs |

## Required Skills & Qualifications

- Recognised expert-level knowledge across Azure, AWS, and GCP — able to design and evaluate architectures on all three platforms
- Proven track record of shaping multi-cloud strategy at enterprise scale (thousands of workloads or significant organizational cloud spend)
- Deep expertise in cloud security, identity, networking, FinOps, AI/ML platforms, and cloud-native application patterns
- Ability to communicate complex architectural concepts clearly to both technical teams and C-level executives
- Strong understanding of enterprise architecture frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman) and how cloud strategy intersects them
- Experience engaging cloud service providers at strategic and advisory levels
- Demonstrated thought leadership — internal publications, architecture communities, or industry presence preferred
- Experience mentoring senior architects and shaping technical culture at scale

**Technology Proficiency Levels:**

**Expert level required:**

- Multi-cloud architecture strategy across Azure, AWS, and GCP at enterprise scale (landing zones, governance, AI/ML, networking, security)
- Cloud governance at scale: policy-as-code, CSPM, and multi-cloud management platforms
- Enterprise architecture frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman, ArchiMate) applied to cloud strategy

**Proficient level required:**

- Infrastructure as Code at scale (Terraform Enterprise/Cloud, Pulumi Enterprise, platform engineering toolchains)
- AI/ML platform architecture across all three providers (Azure AI Foundry, AWS SageMaker, Google Vertex AI)
- Cloud security and identity federation (OIDC, SAML, workload identity federation)

**Working Knowledge required:**

- Cloud financial management strategy and FinOps tooling for multi-year cost modelling
- Enterprise architecture tooling (LeanIX, Sparx EA, ArchiMate) for cloud strategy documentation

**Awareness level expected:**

- Emerging CNAPP, CIEM, and AIOps patterns for next-generation cloud security and operations
- Cloud provider advisory programs (Microsoft MVP, AWS Hero, Google Developer Expert) and technology preview adoption signals

- Authoring or reviewing cloud strategy papers and architecture decision records (ADRs) for major platform decisions
- Meeting with TAL and PAL to align cloud roadmap with organizational priorities and investment plans

- Reviewing the Lead Cloud Architect's cross-platform standards for quality, completeness, and strategic alignment
- Leading or participating in enterprise architecture governance forums
- Evaluating proposed cloud service adoption requests from platform architects against strategic fit
- Mentoring the Lead Cloud Architect and platform architects on strategic thinking and stakeholder communication
- Scanning industry analyst reports, cloud provider announcements, and community research for strategic signals
- Presenting architecture direction and recommendations to senior leadership
